<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You once again play as Nora, a scientist who has been stranded in a surreal, mechanical dimension for years. After living in isolation, Nora discovers a way to finally return to her home (East Berlin). However, she must first confront the Atlas, the rogue artificial intelligence she thought she had escaped.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">🤖 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Immersive Interactions: Everything in the world is physics-based. You can pick up, rotate, and interact with objects to solve puzzles or find resources.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Crafting and Upgrading: Unlike the first game, this sequel introduces a crafting workbench. You can upgrade your weapons (like the classic pistol and rifle) with various attachments and functional improvements.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Combat: Resources are scarce. You have to manage your inventory carefully and decide when to fight the mechanical, biomechanical creatures and when to sneak past them.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stunning Visuals: Utilizing Unreal Engine 5, the game features high-end lighting (Lumen) and incredibly detailed environments that amplify the lonely schizo-tech atmosphere.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming April 29, 2026 to PC.</p>
