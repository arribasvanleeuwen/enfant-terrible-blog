<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Experience an exclusive look at Static Dread The Submarine captured in 4K on an RTX 5090. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">A psychological horror set in a Lovecraftian setting, blending with the cold reality of a military submarine trapped in the depths of the North Atlantic. This story follows ordinary people pushed to the brink of madness as they encounter the unthinkable horrors beneath the waves.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You Are in Charge: Players step into the role of the captain's right hand. Instead of just managing machinery, you must maintain crew discipline, assign specific operational tasks, and manage the human element inside the vessel.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Struggle Against Madness: The crew consists of ordinary people pushed to their absolute limits. As the cosmic dread sets in, you have to monitor their mental states and keep madness from taking over the minds of your crewmates.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hard Choices: The narrative places heavy emphasis on moral dilemmas. Players will frequently face difficult choices where they must decide whether to prioritize completing the dangerous mission or preserving their remaining humanity.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Abyssal Exploration: Survival depends on reading the ocean through sonar signals. The gameplay challenges you to analyze these deep-sea anomalies to determine if a blip on the radar is another military vessel, an equipment glitch, or something ancient and alive.</p>
