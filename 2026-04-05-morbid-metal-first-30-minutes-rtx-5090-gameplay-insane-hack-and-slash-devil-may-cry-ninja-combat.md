  <div class="description-container"><h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Morbid Metal is a fast-paced, solo-developed action RPG that features a unique, seamless character-switching mechanic within a dark, sci-fi world. This brutal roguelite draws inspiration from classic character action games like Devil May Cry to deliver an intense hack-and-slash experience.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in a post-apocalyptic simulation, you play as the "Last AI" created by a mysterious figure known as the Operator. You must fight through waves of lethal machines and colossal bosses to uncover the truth behind humanity's downfall and achieve transcendence. The game features visually striking environments, moving from the overgrown, ruined temples of the Sublime Garden to the industrial, neon-lit decay of the Steel Sanctuary.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Active Shapeshifting: You take a team of up to four distinct characters into a run. Each has a unique weapon (like Flux’s fast blades or Eku’s heavy axe) and specific abilities. Swapping characters allows you to chain together devastating, cross-character combos.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Roguelite Loop: Like Hades, you progress through procedurally generated levels, collecting temporary buffs (Routines and Eden Blessings) and high-risk modifiers (Devil’s Bargains).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Permanent Progression: Between runs, you use "Nano Chips" in a hub area to unlock new characters and permanent skill tree upgrades.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Early Access coming April 8, 2026 to PC.</p>
