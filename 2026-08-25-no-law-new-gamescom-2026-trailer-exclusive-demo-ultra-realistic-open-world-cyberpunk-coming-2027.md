<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Welcome to Port Desire, an industrial cybergrunge city nestled against a cliff overlooking the sea, a high-density port where neon sleaze replaces regulations and everyone wants a piece. You’re Grey Harker, an ex-military veteran attempting to live a quiet life tending to his plants after receiving near-fatal injuries in the field. All you want now is peace, but when trouble comes knocking at your front door, it's time to settle accounts using your black-ops instincts and custom hardware.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">15-20 Hour Campaign: A first person, story-rich single player campaign through the vertical, smog-choked streets of Port Desire (featuring a main story filled with rich sci-fi lore and easter eggs for The Ascent fans).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Open World Immersive Sim: A seamless fusion of sandbox FPS RPG mechanics and immersive-sim depth. Every handcrafted location reacts dynamically to your choices, ensuring no two missions ever unfold the same way.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Total Freedom: Tactical freedom in every situation. Stick to the shadows with stealth, hack into security networks, infiltrate undetected, or give in to unbridled rage with frontal assaults, sci-fi devices, and heavy explosives.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Choices and Consequences: True player agency where actions carry real consequences. Deciding whether to assist an ally or flatline a rival permanently alters the world around you, unlocking alternate story paths across multiple playthroughs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visceral Combat & Upgrades: High-intensity FPS combat featuring vertical movement systems, tactical sci-fi gadgets, and a wide array of military-grade loadout upgrades.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Languages: Fully localized at launch in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Japanese, and Chinese.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Launching in 2027 on PC (Steam, Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
