<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Resonance A Plague Tale Legacy shifts the series from stealth to combat-heavy action in a Mediterranean setting. This native 4K preview showcases Uncharted and Tomb Raider-style traversal mixed with mythic storytelling.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">On the run but determined to uncover the secrets of her past, Sophia's journey leads her to the Minotaur's Island. She must navigate a treacherous maze, face deadly trials, and stay one step ahead of the army chasing her. As enemies are drawn to the island to reclaim a mysterious treasure, a deeper, terrifying challenge awaits: a restless creature hidden behind the myth that will soon resonate with her and the Macula’s mysterious legacy.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">From Stealth to Dynamic Melee: Unlike previous titles in the franchise where you played as vulnerable protagonists hiding in the shadows, you now control a much more capable, physical, and offensive fighter. Survive deadly encounters by relying on your agility, tricks, and sharp reflexes to perfect your parries and unleash powerful strikes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual-Timeline Mechanic: Shift between the Middle Age of Sophia and ancient Minoan times. Experience an unfolding story where the entwined destinies of two heroes are linked by fate, allowing echoes of the past to directly shape the battles and layouts of the present.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Exploration and Light Puzzles: Follow in your crew's footsteps through a richly detailed world, guided by the knowledge safeguarded in your notebook. Solve ingenious puzzles by using a stolen Minoan sphere to manipulate light, a mystical force woven by Daedalus into the island's very heart.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Hunt: A restless presence lurks in the shadows of the island's depths, seemingly knowing your every move. Because every step is a risk and every pause a potential end, you cannot stop; you must hide, outsmart the creature, and push forward to survive.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming August 27, 2026 to PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam / Epic Games Store). It will also be available Day One on Xbox Game Pass.</p>
