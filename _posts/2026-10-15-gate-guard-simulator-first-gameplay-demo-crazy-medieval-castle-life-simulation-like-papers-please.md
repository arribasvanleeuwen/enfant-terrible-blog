<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Exclusive preview of Gate Guard Simulator, an upcoming medieval inspection simulator where you must stop plagues and traitors from entering the King's stronghold.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You are a newly recruited guard serving as the last line of defense against criminals, diseased travelers, and political conspirators trying to enter the kingdom's stronghold. In an age of plagues and political upheaval, every noble, merchant, and peasant must present valid documentation before entering the castle gates. Armed with a halberd, plague masks, and holy water, you must root out counterfeit seals, search merchant carts for hidden contraband, and enforce the King's law, because if the castle burns or the Lord dies of disease, heads will roll.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Authentic Medieval Inspection: Verify entry documents, inspect royal seals, and spot forged crests to ensure only legitimate travelers enter the stronghold.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Halberd & Specialized Tools: Use your trusty halberd to poke through cabbage carts, wear plague masks to spot diseased citizens, and sprinkle holy water on suspicious coffins.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Plague & Threat Prevention: Stay informed with daily news updates to identify wanted convicts, ban suspicious pests, and stop deadly diseases from infecting the Lord's domain.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Wage Management & Tool Upgrades: Spend your hard-earned wages to buy essential inspection gear and specialized equipment to improve your detection efficiency.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Moral Dilemmas & Bribes: Uphold the King's law strictly or look the other way for a generous bribe, your choices directly dictate your pay and the fate of the kingdom.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Brutal Consequences: Mistakes can cause political riots, plague outbreaks, or the Lord's death, ensuring every approval or denial carries severe weight.</p>
