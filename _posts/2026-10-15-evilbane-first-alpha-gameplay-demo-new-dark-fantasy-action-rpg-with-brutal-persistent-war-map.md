<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Exclusive alpha preview of Evilbane, an Unreal Engine 5 co-op action RPG blending Vindictus-style combat with The First Descendant's 4-player boss raids across a dynamic seasonal War Map.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The story of Evilbane begins with a catastrophic war in the Middle Realm over the mysterious power of Heavenstones, leaving thriving kingdoms in ruin. As demonic forces cross over from the Demon Realm and dark Heretics sacrifice innocents for forbidden power, surviving humans gather at Base Camp, their final stronghold. Playing as members of the Resistance, you must deploy from the command center to liberate battlefronts, defend held territories from enemy invasions, and defeat colossal bosses ruling each region to secure blessings and end the dark invasion.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Seasonal War Map: Battle across 2 continents, 10 regions, and 46 territories in a constantly shifting war zone where collective player victories and failures directly push front lines or lose liberated lands.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">4-Player Co-op Action RPG: Assemble a squad or fight solo in intense operations, managing squad synergies to take down deadly demonic forces and colossal regional bosses.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fluid Weapon & Ability Swapping: Freely alternate between melee and ranged gear setups mid-combat to adapt to changing tactical situations and enemy behavior.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Territory Blessings & Base Camp Operations: Reclaim entire regions to unlock shared player blessings, rare regional Heavenstones, and visiting Wandering Merchants at Base Camp.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Persistent Season Progression & War Chronicles: Earn Season Medals, Titles, and historical recognition in Base Camp’s Hall of Records as each Season and Chapter carves your actions into the game's official history.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming Second Half of 2026 to PC (Steam) and 2027 to Xbox Series X|S as a console exclusive.</p>
