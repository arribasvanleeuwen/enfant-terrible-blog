  <div class="description-container">*_Description_*

Tides of Annihilation is a fantasy action adventure RPG inspired by Arthurian legend set in a devastated modern London after a mysterious otherworldly invasion. Players control Gwendolyn, a survivor who gains the ability to summon spectral knights of the Round Table to fight alongside her as she searches for her missing family and uncovers the truth behind the supernatural forces connected to Avalon.

*_Gameplay Features_*

- Spectral Knight Combat: Gwendolyn can summon legendary knights who fight alongside her, adding powerful abilities and combo extensions during combat.

- Large-Scale Boss Encounters: Boss battles are designed as cinematic multi-phase fights that combine arena mechanics, mobility and coordinated attacks.

- Mythic London Setting: Players explore a devastated modern London transformed by supernatural forces connected to the world of Avalon.

- Vertical Exploration: The game allows players to climb, jump and move across large ruined structures and city landmarks.

- Fast-Paced Action Combat: Combat focuses on fluid melee attacks, evasive movement and chaining abilities together in fast, stylish encounters.

Release Date and Platforms: Coming in 2026 to PS5, Xbox and PC.
