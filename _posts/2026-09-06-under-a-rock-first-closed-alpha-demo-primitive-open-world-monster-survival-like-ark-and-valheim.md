Gather resources, build elaborate bases, face local threats, and uncover the mysteries hidden beneath the surface.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Primitive Survival: Players are early 19th-century explorers stranded on a mysterious, uncharted island. The world features an alternate evolutionary path where giant wildlife exists, Neanderthals inhabit the land, and ancient curses are active. Staying true to its primitive survival framework without modern guns or automated systems, progression relies on harvesting raw natural resources, crafting primitive tools and gear, and constructing custom bases while braving dynamic environmental hazards and hostile creatures across surface biomes, underwater areas, and dark caves.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Single Player Focused Testing: Currently running a dedicated single player Alpha build focused on core gameplay stability and island exploration.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tier 1 Survival Systems: Experience essential survival mechanics, including resource harvesting, recipe unlocking, item crafting, and combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanded Island Exploration: Explore a larger map environment beyond the early building-focused tests, featuring diverse biomes and dynamic hazards.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Base Building & Crafting: Construct customized shelters, craft essential gear, and unlock new crafting recipes to advance your survival capabilities.</p>
