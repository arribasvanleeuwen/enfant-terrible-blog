<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">There Are No Ghosts at the Grand is an upcoming first person narrative puzzle adventure game where players take on the role of a fresh hire tasked with restoring the magnificent Grand Hotel to its former glory. Despite the hotel's insistence that nothing supernatural is going on, strange occurrences, flickering lights, and malfunctioning machines tell a different story. Players explore retrofitted wings, solve tactile repair puzzles, and slowly uncover the dark history behind the hotel and its eccentric inhabitants.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Explore the Grand's Arcade Level: Step into the newly revealed Arcade area, filled with retro cabinet games, mechanical oddities, and hidden compartments waiting to be fixed and unlocked.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactile Puzzle-Solving & Repairs: Interact directly with complex mechanical devices, electrical panels, and arcade machines using physical tools and hands-on tinkering to restore power and progress.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Atmospheric Narrative Exploration: Wander through lavish yet decaying halls, uncovering audio logs, letters, and environmental stories that challenge the hotel's official motto: "There are no ghosts."</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unsettling Environmental Mystery: Experience a deeply atmospheric, tense setting where psychological horror meets mystery, balancing quiet exploration with uncanny encounters.</p>
