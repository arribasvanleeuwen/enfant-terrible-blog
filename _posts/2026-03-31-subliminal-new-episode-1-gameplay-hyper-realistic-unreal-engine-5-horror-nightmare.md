<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Developed in Unreal Engine 5, SUBLIMINAL is a first-person puzzle experience that emphasizes environmental interaction and the manipulation of player perception. The first episode introduces core mechanics through minimalist, structured scenarios designed to appeal to fans of titles like Portal and Superliminal.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Subliminal is a psychological horror game inspired by the urban legend of The Backrooms, blending next-gen lighting and rendering with handcrafted, open-ended environments. Set across nostalgic yet unsettling spaces, the experience creates a constant sense that something isn’t quite right, as familiar locations are twisted into something strange and unnatural. With its mix of light and perspective-driven design, unfamiliar presences, and an underlying feeling of decay, Subliminal delivers an eerie and disorienting journey through fragmented, dreamlike environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Psychological Horror Experience: Inspired by the urban legend of The Backrooms, delivering an unsettling and disorienting atmosphere.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Handcrafted Open-Ended Levels: Explore carefully designed environments with a non-linear feel.</p>
