<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This exclusive preview showcases stunning 4K open beta footage of Omega Phenex, an upcoming high-speed indie mecha shooter developed in Unreal Engine 5. The game combines lightning-fast aerial combat with deep mastery mechanics that will highly appeal to fans of Armored Core and Titanfall.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Omega Phenex is a high-speed mech action shooter where you pilot a highly advanced Icarus mech. Born from the developer's desire to create the ultimate mech experience that didn't yet exist, the game plunges players into fierce, tactical battles against massive bosses and relentless enemy armies. Players will seamlessly transition from skimming the ocean surface to soaring high above the clouds in a desperate fight for survival and dominance.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">High-Speed Absolute Mobility: Experience ultimate freedom of movement as you fly, dodge, and maneuver seamlessly across dynamic environments, from wide-open oceans to cloud-filled skies.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Challenging Mech Mastery: Rather than standard, simplified controls, the game challenges you to truly master the intricate mechanics and handling of your advanced Icarus mech.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mechanical Customization System: Changing parts and painting capabilities. The performance of each part varies slightly, but the performance of the mechs is largely determined by the Tune function. It can be used to enhance any parameter of your choice, such as durability value, mobility, or weapon performance by spending a limited predefined amount of the cost. This allows you to customize your mechs to your preferred appearance while pursuing your preferred fighting style.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Massive Boss Battles: Put your piloting skills to the test in white-knuckle combat scenarios against massive bosses and overwhelming enemy forces that require absolute precision.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanding Beta Content & Customization: Engage with a growing arsenal and multiple game modes, including the newly introduced Longsword weapon, the intense Offshore Pursuit Battle mission, and the challenging EX Duel mode.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming in 2027 to PC (Steam).</p>
