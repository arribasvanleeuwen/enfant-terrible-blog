<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fleeing her past while seeking answers to her heritage, Sophia's path brings her to a mysterious island steeped in Minoan legend. To survive, she must brave treacherous environments, navigate deadly trial labyrinths, and evade hostile forces hunting her down. As mercenary factions converge on the island seeking an ancient power, a far greater terror slumbers in the dark, a ancient entity linked directly to the legacy of the Macula.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">From Stealth to Dynamic Melee: Move away from vulnerable hiding mechanics and take control of a highly athletic fighter. Master parries, swift dodges, and devastating counter-attacks in intense melee encounters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual-Timeline Mechanics: Seamlessly shift between medieval times and the ancient Minoan era. Your actions and puzzle solutions in the past ripple into the present, altering paths and combat layouts in real time.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Traversal & Light Puzzles: Scaled vertical cliffs and ancient ruins guided by entries in your field notebook. Utilize a recovered Minoan orb to bend and manipulate light, unlocking ancient mechanisms hidden by Daedalus.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Stalking Presence: A shadowy, relentless presence hunts you across the island, reacting to your every movement. Survival requires moving fast, staying alert, and outsmarting an enemy that never stops pursuing you.</p>
