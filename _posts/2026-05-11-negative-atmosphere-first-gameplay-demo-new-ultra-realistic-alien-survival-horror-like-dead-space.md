<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Negative Atmosphere is an oppressive third person over-the-shoulder shooter survival horror experience centered on dread, tension, and the psychological toll of trauma. Players step into the boots of Dr. Samuel Edwards, the Chief Medical Officer aboard the TRH Rusanov, a massive voidship drifting in deep space. Following a catastrophic outbreak that has mutated the crew into grotesque monstrosities, Edwards must navigate the decaying depths of the ship to reach the Flight Deck.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Atmospheric Survival Horror: A cinematic over-the-shoulder perspective designed to immerse players in an environment where danger is omnipresent and time is a luxury you don't have.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Advanced Medical Crafting: Leverage Samuel’s expertise as a doctor to repurpose routine medications into creative tools of survival, whether for self-augmentation, healing, or influencing enemy behavior.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Morality and Mental State: Every choice interacts with Samuel’s pre-existing beliefs and coping mechanisms. Working for or against his values directly impacts his mental state and alters his reactions to the unfolding horror.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Diverse Mechanical and Organic Threats: Face a variety of distorted former crew-mates and malfunctioning robotic entities that roam the corridors in states of violent instability or panic.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Skulker AI: Experience a sophisticated enemy archetype that refuses to "play fair," forcing you to constantly choose between hiding, fleeing, or engaging in desperate combat.</p>
