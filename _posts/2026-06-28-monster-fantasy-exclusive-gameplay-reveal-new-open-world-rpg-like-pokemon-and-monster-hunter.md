<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">The upcoming premium open world action RPG Monster Fantasy combines inspiration from hits like Pokémon and Monster Hunter to offer a perfect blend of intense creature hunting, monster taming, and relaxing village life.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hunt giant monsters and build a beautiful home! Monster Fantasy is an action RPG with cozy life-sim elements. As an amnesiac hero, explore Eldoras, battle monsters with companions, befriend villagers, and enjoy fishing, cooking, gathering, and crafting.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fight or Build System: The core innovation splits the experience into two independent modules: combat and life simulation. Players can progress through the story by focusing entirely on monster hunting, or they can choose to spend their time gathering, producing, and building homes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Freeform Combat & Classes: Features four distinct classes; Warrior, Mage, Archer, and Swordsman/Martial Artist. Skills are tied directly to each weapon, giving players the freedom to experiment, as a single character can unlock and master every skill in the game.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hunt, Tame, and Evolve: Eldoras is home to around 50 giant monsters, including the Nine-Tailed Fox, Griffon, and Fire Dragon. Defeated monsters can be tamed as pets to aid in exploration and combat. Creatures age over time, evolve new abilities, and can be ridden across biomes like rainforests, icy plains, and volcanoes. Ecological factors like weather and day/night cycles also affect monster behavior.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Life Simulation & Village Management: Players can settle in a beautiful village with around 100 unique NPCs. You can build friendships, work together to create a home, and enjoy activities like fishing, cooking, potion-making, mining, and crafting. Certain villagers can even be recruited to fight alongside you.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Online Multiplayer Co-op: The game fully supports online multiplayer, allowing players to team up with friends to hunt giant creatures, share resources, and build their villages together.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming soon to PC (Steam).</p>
