<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

You play as Jack Pepper, a hard-boiled private investigator (voiced by Troy Baker) and former war veteran. The game is set in the corrupt city of Mouseburg, a place crawling with mobsters, crooked cops, and rival gangs. What starts as a simple "damsel in distress" missing persons case quickly unravels into a massive city-wide conspiracy involving kidnapping and murder.

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

Fast-Paced Combat: Inspired by classics like Doom and Wolfenstein. You’ll use a variety of weapons, from classic Tommy guns and shotguns to cartoonish explosives like dynamite and even power-ups like spinach or cheese to boost your abilities.

Detective Work: Unlike traditional shooters, you’ll spend time investigating crime scenes, gathering clues, and solving puzzles to progress through the story.

Upgrades and Exploration: You can upgrade your arsenal using found blueprints and explore a semi-open hub world in Mouseburg, which includes diverse locations like film studios, sewers, and casinos.

Traversal: Jack has access to advanced movement tools like a grappling hook and wall-running to navigate the environment and outmaneuver enemies.

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

Coming April 16, 2026 to PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch and Switch 2.
