The entire game is presented in black and white with hand-drawn, frame-by-frame animations, creating a vintage cartoon aesthetic that contrasts sharply with its gritty, violent boomer shooter gameplay.</p>
<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>
<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mouse P.I. For Hire is an upcoming noir-inspired "boomer shooter" that blends gritty, first-person gameplay with a striking 1930s hand-drawn animation style. This early Unity 6 footage showcases a black-and-white aesthetic reminiscent of classic cartoons and Cuphead.</p>
<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>
<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You play as Jack Pepper, a hard-boiled private investigator (voiced by Troy Baker) and former war veteran. The game is set in the corrupt city of Mouseburg, a place crawling with mobsters, crooked cops, and rival gangs. What starts as a simple "damsel in distress" missing persons case quickly unravels into a massive city-wide conspiracy involving kidnapping and murder.</p>
<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>
<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fast-Paced Combat: Inspired by classics like Doom and Wolfenstein. You’ll use a variety of weapons, from classic Tommy guns and shotguns to cartoonish explosives like dynamite and even power-ups like spinach or cheese to boost your abilities.</p>
<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Detective Work: Unlike traditional shooters, you’ll spend time investigating crime scenes, gathering clues, and solving puzzles to progress through the story.</p>
<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Upgrades and Exploration: You can upgrade your arsenal using found blueprints and explore a semi-open hub world in Mouseburg, which includes diverse locations like film studios, sewers, and casinos.</p>
<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Traversal: Jack has access to advanced movement tools like a grappling hook and wall-running to navigate the environment and outmaneuver enemies.</p>
<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>
<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming April 16, 2026 to PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch and Switch 2.</p>
