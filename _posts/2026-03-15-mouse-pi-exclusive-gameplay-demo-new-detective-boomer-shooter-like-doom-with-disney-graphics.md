<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 25px; margin: 30px 0; border-radius: 8px; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic; font-size: 1.05em; line-height: 1.7;">Mouse P.I. For Hire is an upcoming noir-inspired "boomer shooter" that blends gritty, first-person gameplay with a striking 1930s hand-drawn animation style. This early Unity 6 footage showcases a black-and-white aesthetic reminiscent of classic cartoons and Cuphead.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 40px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 10px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 25px; line-height: 1.8; color: #f0f0f0;">You play as Jack Pepper, a hard-boiled private investigator (voiced by Troy Baker) and former war veteran. The game is set in the corrupt city of Mouseburg, a place crawling with mobsters, crooked cops, and rival gangs. What starts as a simple "damsel in distress" missing persons case quickly unravels into a massive city-wide conspiracy involving kidnapping and murder.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 40px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 10px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<div style="margin-bottom: 12px; padding-left: 20px; position: relative; line-height: 1.6; color: #e0e0e0;"><span style="color: #FF0B55; position: absolute; left: 0; font-weight: bold;">•</span>Fast-Paced Combat: Inspired by classics like Doom and Wolfenstein. You’ll use a variety of weapons, from classic Tommy guns and shotguns to cartoonish explosives like dynamite and even power-ups like spinach or cheese to boost your abilities.</div>

<div style="margin-bottom: 12px; padding-left: 20px; position: relative; line-height: 1.6; color: #e0e0e0;"><span style="color: #FF0B55; position: absolute; left: 0; font-weight: bold;">•</span>Detective Work: Unlike traditional shooters, you’ll spend time investigating crime scenes, gathering clues, and solving puzzles to progress through the story.</div>

<div style="margin-bottom: 12px; padding-left: 20px; position: relative; line-height: 1.6; color: #e0e0e0;"><span style="color: #FF0B55; position: absolute; left: 0; font-weight: bold;">•</span>Upgrades and Exploration: You can upgrade your arsenal using found blueprints and explore a semi-open hub world in Mouseburg, which includes diverse locations like film studios, sewers, and casinos.</div>

<div style="margin-bottom: 12px; padding-left: 20px; position: relative; line-height: 1.6; color: #e0e0e0;"><span style="color: #FF0B55; position: absolute; left: 0; font-weight: bold;">•</span>Traversal: Jack has access to advanced movement tools like a grappling hook and wall-running to navigate the environment and outmaneuver enemies.</div>
