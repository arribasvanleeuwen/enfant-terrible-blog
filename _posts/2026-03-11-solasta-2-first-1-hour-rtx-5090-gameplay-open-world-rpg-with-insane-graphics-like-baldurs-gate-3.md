<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This Unreal Engine 5 preview of Solasta II highlights a hardcore tactical RPG that remains faithful to the 2024 D&amp;D ruleset. It offers a deep, turn-based strategy experience with dice-driven mechanics, designed for fans of modern classics like Baldur's Gate 3 and Divinity: Original Sin 2.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Solasta II is the upcoming sequel to the acclaimed tactical RPG Solasta: Crown of the Magister, developed by Tactical Adventures. It is widely recognized for its strict adherence to tabletop rules and its deep, grid-based tactical combat.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Initial Content: The Early Access version will include a significant portion of Act I, offering roughly 10–15 hours of gameplay, with a level cap of 4 (set to increase to level 10 for the 1.0 release).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">SRD 5.2 Ruleset: Solasta II is one of the first major titles to implement the updated 2024 D&amp;D rules (via the SRD 5.2). This includes the new Weapon Mastery system, giving non-casters more tactical depth in combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unreal Engine 5: Moving away from an older version of Unity, the sequel uses a new engine to provide much more detailed environments, better lighting (crucial for its "light vs. dark" mechanics), and improved character models.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Depth: It retains the signature verticality of the first game, where positioning on high ground and interacting with the environment (pushing boulders, dropping chandeliers) is key to winning battles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Land of Neokos: A mana-rich realm currently being corrupted by an ancient, shadowy force led by an enigmatic entity named Shadwyn.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Colwall Siblings: Instead of a fully custom party of strangers, the story follows four adoptive siblings (though you still customize their classes and appearances). This change allows for a more personal, cinematic narrative with a high-profile voice cast including Amelia Tyler (narrator of Baldur's Gate 3) and Ben Starr.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Moral Alignment: While the game follows a heroic path, your choices regarding different factions will determine how the world of Neokos evolves.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Classes and Races: At the start of Early Access, players can choose from 6 classes (Fighter, Cleric, Wizard, Rogue, Sorcerer, and Paladin) and 4 ancestries (Human, Elf, Dwarf, and Halfling).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">4-Player Co-op: The game supports full drop-in/drop-out cooperative play from day one.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Partially Open World: Unlike the linear maps of the first game, Solasta II features a world map with dynamic events, random encounters, and timed side-quests.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Early Access coming March 12, 2026 to PC.</p>
