  <div class="description-container">*_Description:_*

Solasta II is the upcoming sequel to the acclaimed tactical RPG Solasta: Crown of the Magister, developed by Tactical Adventures. It is widely recognized for its strict adherence to tabletop rules and its deep, grid-based tactical combat.

*_Gameplay Features:_*

- Initial Content: The Early Access version will include a significant portion of Act I, offering roughly 10–15 hours of gameplay, with a level cap of 4 (set to increase to level 10 for the 1.0 release).

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">- SRD 5.2 Ruleset: Solasta II is one of the first major titles to implement the updated 2024 D&amp;D rules (via the SRD 5.2). This includes the new Weapon Mastery system, giving non-casters more tactical depth in combat.</h3>

- Unreal Engine 5: Moving away from an older version of Unity, the sequel uses a new engine to provide much more detailed environments, better lighting (crucial for its "light vs. dark" mechanics), and improved character models.

- Tactical Depth: It retains the signature verticality of the first game, where positioning on high ground and interacting with the environment (pushing boulders, dropping chandeliers) is key to winning battles.

- The Land of Neokos: A mana-rich realm currently being corrupted by an ancient, shadowy force led by an enigmatic entity named Shadwyn.

- The Colwall Siblings: Instead of a fully custom party of strangers, the story follows four adoptive siblings (though you still customize their classes and appearances). This change allows for a more personal, cinematic narrative with a high-profile voice cast including Amelia Tyler (narrator of Baldur's Gate 3) and Ben Starr.

- Moral Alignment: While the game follows a heroic path, your choices regarding different factions will determine how the world of Neokos evolves.

- Classes and Races: At the start of Early Access, players can choose from 6 classes (Fighter, Cleric, Wizard, Rogue, Sorcerer, and Paladin) and 4 ancestries (Human, Elf, Dwarf, and Halfling).

- 4-Player Co-op: The game supports full drop-in/drop-out cooperative play from day one.

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">- Partially Open World: Unlike the linear maps of the first game, Solasta II features a world map with dynamic events, random encounters, and timed side-quests.</h3>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">*_Release Date and Platforms:_* Early Access coming March 12, 2026 to PC.</h3>
