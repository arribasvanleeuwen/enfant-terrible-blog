<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This exclusive Unreal Engine 5 gameplay preview showcases the physics-driven medieval zombie survival simulator God Save Birmingham running in 4K on an RTX 5090, highlighting the new co-op mode.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Judgment Day has come to medieval Birmingham, and a plague of flesh-hungry ghouls has claimed every living soul… except one. Fight to survive in a highly tactile, physics-based survival sim where every object can be manipulated. Make ends meet, or meet your end.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND NEW BETA CONTENT</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Closed Beta Sandbox: Experience a solo survival sandbox featuring a tutorial priory area and parts of the village, including the historic Moor Street and St. Martin's Church.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Physics-Driven Combat & Environment: Use traditional weapons like axes and spears, or get creative by throwing and kicking everyday objects like buckets, chairs, and ladders to trip up the horde. Be careful, tripping over ledges or falling can cause serious injury, like broken legs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Crafting, Cooking & Barricading: Level up your carpentry, tailoring, and blacksmithing to craft full armor sets. Cook hearty meals like pottage to stay fed, and fortify your chosen shelter by installing door latches, building fences, and placing barricades.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-Op Multiplayer: Play with up to 4 players, featuring dedicated servers, respawning after death, and a survival leaderboard.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Save/Load System: Added an auto-save slot that saves when sleeping or exiting to the main menu/closing the server.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Smarter Infected: Zombies can now vault low obstacles, open windows, and break down locked doors.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Character Customization: Added a character editor to create unique physical appearances for co-op play.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanded Survival & Crafting: Introduced clothing insulation for body temperature management, inventory weight reduction for equipped gear, and new weapons, recipes, and barricades.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">UI & Quality of Life Improvements: Overhauled the inventory with filters, redesigned the survival journal, added key rebinding, multi-use actions, and visual status indicators (moodles) for fatigue, cold, and hygiene.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming soon in Early Access to PC (Steam). PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S planned for the full release.</p>
