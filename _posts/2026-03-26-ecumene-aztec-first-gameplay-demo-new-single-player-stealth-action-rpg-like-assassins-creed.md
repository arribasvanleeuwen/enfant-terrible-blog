<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This upcoming third-person survival RPG, set during the 16th-century Spanish conquest, features an Aztec warrior defending his city through a blend of stealth and brutal combat. Developed in Unreal Engine 5, the game combines historical realism with mythological elements to create an experience reminiscent of titles like Assassin's Creed.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Ecumene Aztec is a brutal action adventure RPG game with stealth elements, set in the time of the Aztecs. The year is 1521. The Spanish conquistadors led by Hernán Cortés are burning Tenochtitlan, but they are not the only threat. In the shadows of the conflict, betrayal, deception and a struggle for power unfold. As the survivor of a ritual sacrifice, you must decide whether to side with the Aztecs in defence of their traditions, or join those who seek to destroy the ancient empire.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Diverse Character Classes: You can choose between three distinct archetypes, each inspired by Aztec culture: the Jaguar (focuses on brute strength and direct combat), the Eagle (specializes in agility and aerial maneuvers) and the Snake (prioritizes stealth, traps, and poison).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Survival Mechanics: Players must navigate a dense, hostile jungle environment. This involves crafting equipment, managing resources, and utilizing the terrain to ambush technologically superior enemies.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stealth and Guerilla Warfare: Since the Spanish invaders possess steel armor and firearms, the game emphasizes staying in the shadows. You’ll use the "Great City" and the surrounding wilderness as a playground for tactical takedowns.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Blood Sacrifices and Rituals: In keeping with the historical and mythological context, players can offer sacrifices to the gods to gain supernatural boons or upgrades to their physical abilities.</p>
