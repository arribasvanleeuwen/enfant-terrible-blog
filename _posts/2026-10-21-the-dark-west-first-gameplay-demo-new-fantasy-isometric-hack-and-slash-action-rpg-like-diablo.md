<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Dark West is an occult western Action RPG set in a dying frontier consumed by darkness and corruption. Set in the late 19th century where faith and damnation clash under an eternal dusk, players must navigate a grim world where greed has unearthed foul forces. Armed with era-specific weaponry and dark magic, you fight to survive against cursed enemies and uncover powerful tools of destruction.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Western Weaponry & Dark Magic: Combine period-accurate firearms, such as shotguns, rifles, and revolvers, with occult abilities to overcome deadly threats.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep ARPG Progression & Builds: Customize your character with skill trees, gear upgrades, craftable items, and dynamic character builds tailored to your preferred playstyle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Consumable & Buff Systems: Utilize specialized items like alcohol and potions found in locations like the Double Deuce Saloon or out in the wild to gain temporary combat bonuses such as increased damage, movement speed, or critical strikes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Grim Frontier Exploration: Traverse atmospheric environments, from town saloons to corrupted flatlands, and face challenging boss encounters and dangerous bounties.</p>
