<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Experience The Expanse: Osiris Reborn in native 4K, showcasing Unreal Engine 5 combat and zero-G exploration on a remote space station. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Expanse: Osiris Reborn is a story-driven action RPG set in The Expanse universe with focus on companions, choices and dynamic third-person combat. You play as a Pinkwater Security employee who survives a catastrophic incident on Eros and who has to build a team to fight back against Protogen and their experiments. The game is set in the same universe as the books and show, but focuses on its own story and characters.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 CLOSED BETA FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Access Requirements: The beta is available starting April 22nd exclusively for supporters who purchased the Miller’s Pack or the Collector’s Edition.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Character Customization: Players can choose between a male Earther or a female Belter, with the ability to specialize as either an Officer or a Hacker.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Story and Setting: The beta features one early-game story mission set on the Pinkwater-4 space station, taking place shortly after the protagonist and their sibling escape from Eros.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Combat: Gameplay is built around a cover-and-repositioning system. It includes an active pause feature to assess the battlefield and a loadout consisting of one primary weapon and one sidearm.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Core Mechanics: Players can experience zero-G movement, exploration of remote locations, and interaction with various NPCs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Progression Systems: Weapons and equipment can be modified at workbenches using scavenged materials.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Skills: Both combat and social skills can be improved to refine specific playstyles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Choice and Consequence: The mission includes a decision-making system where choices made during conversations and high-tension moments result in both immediate and long-term consequences.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The game is coming Spring 2027 to PS5, Xbox and PC and will launch on Xbox Game Pass Day One.</p>
