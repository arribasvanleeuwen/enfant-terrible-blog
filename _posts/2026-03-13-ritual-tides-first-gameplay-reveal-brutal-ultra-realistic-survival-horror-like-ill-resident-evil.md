  <div class="description-container">*_Description:_*

Ritual Tides is an upcoming first person survival horror game set in the 1800s. The game takes place on Hortus, a desolate British island. Players begin the journey washed up on its unforgiving shores, forced to navigate decaying settlements, bleak landscapes and environmental mystery; uncovering why the island was abandoned and what remains in the shadows.

*_Gameplay Features:_*

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">- AAA Visual Fidelity: Experience a world designed to surpass the graphical standards of modern blockbuster titles, focusing on extreme environmental detail and photorealistic immersion.</h3>

- Custom First Person Immersion: Built from the ground up, the game features entirely custom first-person systems designed to make navigating the island of Hortus feel tactile and atmospheric.

- Tension-First Combat: Eschewing traditional "run-and-gun" shooter tropes, the combat mechanics are uniquely engineered to prioritize dread, weight, and survival over high-action gameplay.

- Narrative-Heavy Scripting: Immerse yourself in a deeply crafted story written in collaboration with acclaimed horror novelist Adam Nevill (The Ritual), ensuring a high-caliber literary horror experience.
