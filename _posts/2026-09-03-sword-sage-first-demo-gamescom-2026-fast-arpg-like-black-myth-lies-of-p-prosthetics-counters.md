<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Immersing players in a realm shattered by ancient forbidden knowledge, Sword Sage: Awakening follows Pei Sanniang, a rookie Yaoguai Vanquisher from the Yuangong Sect. Facing natural disasters and monstrous infestations caused by the leaked celestial books, players must master fluid swordsmanship, Taoist magic, and mechanical artifacts to confront undocumented horrors and restore balance to Jiannandao.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Next-Gen Mythological World: Built from the ground up in Unreal Engine 5 to deliver cinematic visuals, dynamic lighting, and a unique amber-punk fantasy aesthetic.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Evade and Strike Combat: A signature directional counter-based system where players intercept enemy attacks, break postures, and strike back with martial arts and Taoist spells.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Weapon Mechanics: Seamlessly blend dual-blade strikes, leg combos, and specialized prosthetic tools while managing primary blades that can shatter and re-forge mid-battle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Terrifying Yaoguai Encounters: Battle massive folkloric beasts and intricate boss threats that demand pattern recognition and precise tactical adaptation.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Folkloric Lore: Embark on a journey across Jiannandao, uncovering ancient Zhiguai mysteries, political secrets, and colossal mechanical wonders.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming soon to PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
