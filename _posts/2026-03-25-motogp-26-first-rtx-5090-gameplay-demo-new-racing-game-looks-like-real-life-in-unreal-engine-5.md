<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">MotoGP 26 brings the official MotoGP experience, featuring licensed riders, teams, bikes, and circuits from the championship. Players can build their career, progress through the season, and experience realistic riding mechanics based on rider movement and bike control. The game also includes additional disciplines such as Motard, Minibikes, and Flat Track, alongside online multiplayer modes.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Official 2026 Championship: Step into the full MotoGP™ 2026 season with all official riders, teams, bikes, and tracks. Rider performance evolves during the season, as real-world results influence their in-game ratings.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Redesigned Physics System: The riding model has been reworked to focus more on how the rider moves on the bike, making weight shifting and body positioning more important than ever.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Arcade & Pro Experience: You can choose between a more accessible Arcade style or a deeper simulation with Pro mode, both supported by tutorials, adaptive difficulty, and riding assists.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Career Mode Evolution: Build your own career from rookie to legend or relive the journey of an official rider, with contracts, team relationships, and media interactions shaping your path.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Race Off & Training: Take a break from race weekends and try different disciplines like Motard, Flat Track, and Minibikes, along with production bikes featuring their own handling style.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Multiplayer & Cross-play: Jump into online races with full crossplay support, compete in 22-rider grids, and customize your look with advanced editors you can share with others.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>
