  <div class="description-container">*_Description:_*

Nakwon is a multiplayer zombie survival extraction game set in a devastated city overrun by the infected. Players begin as refugees living in a survivor shelter and must venture into the dangerous urban districts to search for food, materials and equipment needed to stay alive. Every expedition is a high-risk mission where zombies and other survivors compete for the same limited resources. Players must maintain their shelter, craft equipment and prepare for dangerous scavenging runs.

*_Gameplay Features:_*

- Urban Open World: Explore a city in South Korea overrun by the infected while searching for vital resources.

- PvPvE Expeditions: Face both zombies and other survivors during high-risk missions across dangerous urban zones.

- Day and Night Survival Cycle: Manage your shelter during the day and venture into the city at night to scavenge for supplies.

- Strategic Survival Choices: Players can hide, escape, cooperate or betray others depending on the situation.

- Shelter Management: Build and customize your refuge with furniture, craft items and upgrade your equipment to survive longer.

- Realistic Survival Systems: Everyday objects can become weapons, small wounds can be fatal and psychological stress can affect your survival.
