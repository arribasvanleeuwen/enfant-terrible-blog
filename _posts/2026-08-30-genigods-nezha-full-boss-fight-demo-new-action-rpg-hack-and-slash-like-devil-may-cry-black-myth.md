<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Grounded in ancient Chinese creation mythology, Genigods: Nezha puts players in the shoes of the Spirit Pearl (the first life forged by Nüwa, the Goddess of Life, from earth, water, and fire), reborn as Nezha to fight colossal gods and restore a fractured world.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hardcore Action Combat: Features dynamic, fast-paced melee and magic combat against massive mythical entities.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Colossal Boss Battles: Epic boss fights inspired by Shadow of the Colossus and God of War, where players must traverse mountain-sized enemies to target weak points.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual Stances & Mobility: Seamlessly transition between ground-based combat and dynamic aerial movement using Nezha's iconic Wind Fire Wheels.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mystic & Elemental Abilities: Shift forms, such as turning into water to dodge incoming attacks or instantly close distances, to outmaneuver powerful bosses.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Ancient Chinese Mythology: Features rich lore centered around Nüwa, celestial deities, and ancient genesis legends.</p>
