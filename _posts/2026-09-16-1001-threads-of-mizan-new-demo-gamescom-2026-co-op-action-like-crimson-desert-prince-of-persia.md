  <div class="description-container"><h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">1001 Threads of Mizan is an upcoming 1-to-3 player co-op action adventure from former BioWare and Ubisoft veterans that blends high-speed flying carpet combat with rich Arabian folklore.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Inspired by the classic folklore of One Thousand and One Nights, 1001 Threads of Mizan is a 1-to-3 player cooperative action adventure game set in a vibrant, sprawling Arabian fantasy world. Players join forces with the legendary storyweaver Shahrazad to unearth ancient lost technology, uncover a tale of betrayal, and protect humanity from a looming jinn incursion.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-op Action: Play solo or team up online as heroes Yusra, Malik, or Sufyan. The hero synergy is a standout, combining Yusra's swift agility, Malik's heavy strikes, and Sufyan's crowd control makes team coordination feel genuinely rewarding rather than optional.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Aerial Carpet Traversal & Combat: Seamlessly weave movement and combat together using nimble flying carpets. The flight controls are remarkably weightless and intuitive. Diving into dunes to gain speed and maneuvering around ruins feels like a core combat mechanic rather than just a gimmick.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Colossal Jinn Boss Battles: Face off against towering mythic beasts in 360-degree vertical space. The Jinn Sand Knight fight was the highlight of our demo; fighting in mid-air forces fantastic teamwork, where one player baiting aggro while others attack weak points creates an amazing spectacle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Vibrant Living World: Explore mythic desert landscapes and ruins buried in the sand. The gorgeous Arabian fantasy art style and rich atmosphere make 1001 Threads of Mizan one of the most promising upcoming co-op surprises.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Release date to be announced. Coming to PC, PS5, XBOX and Switch 2.</p>
