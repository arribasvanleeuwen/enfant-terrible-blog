  <div class="description-container"><p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Developed in Unreal Engine 5 by Saber Interactive with direct input from Clive Barker, Hellraiser Revival is an ambitious gore first person survival horror game that follows biker gang member Aidan Lynch as he navigates the hellish, labyrinthine dimension of the Cenobites to rescue his girlfriend.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Clive Barker's Hellraiser Revival is a cinematic, first person action survival horror game that leans heavily into extreme physical and psychological dread. The story kicks off when Aidan Lynch inadvertently activates a supernatural puzzle box during an intimate encounter with his girlfriend Sunny, summoning Pinhead and his sadomasochistic followers. After Sunny is taken to Hell, players must dive headfirst into the intensely violent world of body horror where the line between ultimate pleasure and agonizing pain is completely erased.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Uncompromised Mature Themes and Body Horror: The game features explicit gore and heavy atmosphere of provocative sadomasochism. The environments are covered in grotesque details, and the narrative explores a messed-up love story where the themes of Clive Barker's original mythos are fully realized.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Wielding the Puzzle Box (Genesis/Lament Configuration): A standout mechanic is the active use of the supernatural puzzle box. Moving beyond a simple story item, players use the cube in real-time combat to unleash extra-dimensional abilities. The telekinetic combat loop is great. Players can actively pluck fire from torches or rip saw blades out of walls to launch them at enemies, turning the environment itself into a weapon at the cost of the box's charge.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Grounded, Fragile Combat and Immersive Sim Elements: Playing as Aidan, a regular guy with zero formal weapon training, firearms and melee combat feel deliberately clumsy, heavy, and desperate. Melee weapons like baseball bats and knives are fully breakable, forcing players to adapt. The game features immersive sim-lite mechanics, offering tight arena spaces with multiple pathways. Players can choose to sneak past horrific threats entirely, look for hidden notes with keypad codes, or lure enemies into carefully positioned bear traps.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Facing the Scarlet Church and Cenobites: The gameplay showcases terrifying enemy variety, forcing players to defend against hostile cultists of the Scarlet Church and the iconic, horrific Cenobites. While blunt weapons smash armor and sharp blades cut unprotected flesh, we can confirm that certain supernatural entities cannot be defeated through standard combat, requiring players to run, hide, and manage scarce resources to survive the nightmarish encounters.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 8, 2026 to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam). Standard and Deluxe editions will be available alongside premium physical Collector's Editions featuring a replica of the puzzle box.</p>
