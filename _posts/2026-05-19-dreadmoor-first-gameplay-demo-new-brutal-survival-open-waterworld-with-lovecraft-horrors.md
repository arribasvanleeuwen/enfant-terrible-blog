<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This exclusive pre-alpha gameplay demo showcases Dreadmoor, an upcoming Lovecraftian open world fishing adventure running on Unreal Engine 5. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dreadmoor is a dark first person fishing adventure game set in a submerged world, rotting from a past catastrophe. Fish in murky waters, upgrade your boat, craft survival gear, and fight monstrous creatures that lurk under the water and on the land.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Core Loop: Players navigate corrupted, mysterious waters to catch anomalous fish and strange yields, which can be sold to shadowy merchants and local survivors for profit.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Upgrades and Crafting: Money and resources earned from your catches are used to upgrade your vessel, craft essential survival tools, and manage a grid-based inventory system.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Surviving the Environment: The world is highly hostile. Players must manage critical survival meters, maintain their ship, and safely navigate harsh, unpredictable environmental conditions out on the water.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Action and Combat: Unlike passive fishing games, players must actively defend themselves in first person against terrifying mutated creatures and cosmic horrors lurking beneath the surface and on land.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mystery and Psychological Horror: The game features a deep, narrative-driven exploration of forgotten lands, ancient secrets, and psychological dread, where even fellow survivors cannot be fully trusted.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming Q4 2026 to PC.</p>
