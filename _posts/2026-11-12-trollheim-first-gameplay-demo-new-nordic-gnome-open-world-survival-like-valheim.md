<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This overview showcases Trollheim, featuring core mechanics from the tiny-gnome survival adventure set in the procedural Dark Forest, alongside base-building and craftable gear.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Explore an ancient, living forest as a tiny gnome in this co-op PVE survival crafting adventure inspired by Nordic folklore. Gather, craft and build a home for your gnome family amongst the treetops, and encounter the Väsen, mythical beings both friendly and dangerous.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Nordic Folklore & The Väsen: Encounter mythical beings known as the Väsen. Some creatures (such as certain trolls, faeries, or sirens) may sit by your campfire and join your village, while others will attack on sight.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Procedural Dark Forest Sandbox: Explore an ancient, living forest. Play solo offline or collaborate with up to 7 friends (8 players total) in a procedurally grown world.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Gathering & Crafting Systems: Adapt to environmental threats, cold, and hunger. Gather blueberries and twigs to craft essential gear, tools, arrows, and even musical instruments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Treetop & Wilderness Base Building: Construct your home wherever you choose, high up in treetops, inside hollowed logs, tucked into rock crevices, or beside misty forest lakes. Customize your structures with bridges, mossy walls, and low ambient lighting.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-op PvE Survival Mechanics: Work together to establish a thriving gnome community, build shelter against the elements, and navigate the mysterious threats spreading through the woods.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Trollheim is planned for release in Early Access on PC (Steam).</p>
