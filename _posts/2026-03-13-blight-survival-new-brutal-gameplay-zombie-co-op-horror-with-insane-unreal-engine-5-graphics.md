  <div class="description-container">*_Description:_*

Blight: Survival is a PvE solo or co-operative horror game where you take down monsters with medieval weaponry. The game takes place in the deadly "no man's land" between two kingdoms locked in a ceaseless war. In this purgatory, a devastating new plague called the Blight has emerged, fueled by the blood of the fallen, turning the infected into monsters (essentially medieval zombies). You and your kin, the Writhens, are the only ones willing to venture forth to eradicate the all-consuming Blight at its source.

*_Gameplay Features:_*

- Co-op extraction-lite with roguelite elements, supporting up to 4 players.

- Players undertake missions, battling both humans (bandits) and the Blinded (monsters).

- The combat is described as visceral, directional, and skill-based, allowing for high-skill gameplay.

- Looting and Extraction are key: You must collect resources, weapons, and armor, and then decide whether to secure your spoils by returning to your camp or risking it all to push further.

- You can customize your character with a diverse range of weapons, armor, and talents.
