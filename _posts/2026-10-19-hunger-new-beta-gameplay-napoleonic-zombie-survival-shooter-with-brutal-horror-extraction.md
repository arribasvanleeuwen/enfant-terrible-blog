<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hunger is an upcoming multiplayer first person extraction RPG set in the dark, chaotic aftermath of a plague-ravaged Napoleonic Europe. A bacterium of unknown origin has twisted the continent into a desolate wasteland filled with ruin, fear, and unspeakable horrors. Players step into the shoes of unique survivors venturing out on risky expeditions to scavenge resources, build their reputation, and outlast both terrifying monsters and rival survivors.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visceral Guns & Steel Combat: Master a methodical combat system combining black-powder firearms (flintlocks, muskets, and hand mortars) with heavy melee combat. Fluidly switch from long-range volleys to brutal sabers, axes, and maces, where stamina, parrying, and positioning dictate survival.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">PvPvE Extraction: Venture into plague-ravaged European ruins to scavenge for vital resources. Face terrifying mutated monstrosities while navigating unpredictable encounters with rival players and fight to the death to secure your loot.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Permanent RPG Progression with No Mandatory Wipes: Build your survivors without losing your hard-earned progress. Deep mastery trees with over 100 talents, alongside unlockable Passive, Tactical, and Ultimate abilities, allow you to craft specialized builds, from stealthy solo scavengers to heavy squad anchors.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Chateau & Player-Driven Economy: Return between runs to a lively multiplayer hub. Trade through a player-driven auction house, craft high-tier gear, accept contracts from faction NPCs, and interact with other players in real-time via proximity VOIP.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hardcore Endgame Raids & Bosses: Push beyond standard extractions into lethal multi-zone dungeons. Face extreme environmental hazards and nightmarish boss encounters that demand high-level gear, tight team coordination, and strategic execution.</p>
