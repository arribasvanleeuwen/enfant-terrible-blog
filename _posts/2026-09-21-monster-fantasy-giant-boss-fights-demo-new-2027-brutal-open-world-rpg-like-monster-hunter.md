<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Captured in 4K on an RTX 5090, this exclusive alpha footage showcases Monster Fantasy, an upcoming Unreal Engine 5 open world action RPG and cozy life sim that blends giant monster hunting, creature taming, and village management in a colorful Chibi aesthetic.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Monster Fantasy is an action RPG with cozy life-sim elements. As an amnesiac hero awakening in Eldoras, explore diverse biomes, battle giant monsters with companions, befriend villagers, and enjoy farming, fishing, cooking, gathering, and crafting.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fight or Build System: The core innovation splits the experience into two independent modules: combat and life simulation. Players can progress through the story by focusing entirely on monster hunting, or they can choose to spend their time gathering, producing, farming, and building homes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Classless Weapon-Based Combat: Instead of locked classes, skills are tied directly to equipped weapons, such as Greatsword, Sword & Shield, Dual Blades, Bow, or Magic Staff, giving players full freedom to experiment and master every skill on a single character.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hunt, Tame, and Evolve: Eldoras is home to over 50 giant monsters, including the Nine-Tailed Fox, Griffon, and Fire Dragon. Defeated monsters can be tamed as pets to aid in exploration, fight in combat, serve as mounts, or assist with chores around your village. Creatures age over time, evolve new abilities, and react dynamically to environmental factors like weather and day/night cycles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Life Simulation & Village Management: Players can settle in a beautiful village with around 100 unique NPCs. You can build friendships, work together to create a home, and enjoy activities like farming, fishing, cooking, potion-making, mining, and crafting. Certain villagers can even be recruited to fight alongside you.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Online Multiplayer Co-op: The game fully supports online multiplayer, allowing players to team up with friends to hunt giant creatures, share resources, and build their villages together.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Currently in pre-alpha development. Coming in 2027 to PC (Steam).</p>
