<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Reanimal The Prisoner is the first chapter in The Expanded World DLC campaign (a three-part expansion that broadens this new dark and horrifying world). Uncover long-dead secrets, cast new light on old friends, and discover how deep the roots of violence truly go. In this new story, players control two new playable characters, The Prisoner and The Soldier, as they run from a relentless new enemy: the Flamethrower Soldier. Playable in single player as well as couch and online co-op, the experience offers 60–90 minutes of gameplay depending on playstyle, and includes a Friend's Pass so you can share the journey.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">New Playable Characters: Control two distinct new characters, The Prisoner and The Soldier.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">New Tools: Utilize the Instant Camera and the Axe to navigate hazards and threats.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">New Relentless Enemy: Face off against the menacing Flamethrower Soldier.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unique Mechanics: Learn how to travel by corpse, ride a motorcycle, and avoid deadly Claymore mines.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Revisited Locations: Rediscover familiar places from the main story and encounter recognizable faces.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-Op Support & Friend's Pass: Playable in single player or co-op (couch and online) featuring a Friend's Pass.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming August 7, 2026 to PS5, XBOX, PC and Switch 2.</p>
