<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Serving as a prequel expansion to Cronos: The New Dawn, Cronos: Lazarus shifts focus from slow-burn survival horror to a faster, more aggressive combat loop. Players take control of the Pathfinder on his dark journey to becoming the iconic Warden, wielding new weapons, powerful traversal abilities, and decoy projection mechanics while defying the Collective to revive a lost Essence.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Faster Combat: Shifts away from the slow-paced survival horror of the base game to feature a more aggressive playstyle, equipped with heavy-hitting weaponry like the Gladius and expanded ability sets.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Relentless Tracker Encounter: Introduces a persistent, highly adaptive stalker enemy specifically engineered by the Collective to hunt down the Warden and match his immense power. The shift toward direct action feels refreshing and empowering. The intense cat-and-mouse dynamic created by the new Tracker enemy is great.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Decoy & Traversal Mechanics: Features tactical combat upgrades, including holographic projection decoys to distract enemies and enhanced movement for fast-paced arena navigation.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Origin Story of the Warden: Dives deep into the lore to reveal the hidden story, human struggles, and undisclosed secrets behind one of the franchise's most recognizable characters.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming September 5, 2026 to PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
