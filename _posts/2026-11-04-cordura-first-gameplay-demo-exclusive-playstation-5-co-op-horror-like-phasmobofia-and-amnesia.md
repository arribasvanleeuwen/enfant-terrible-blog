<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Descend into shifting Victorian labyrinths for a lucrative yet deadly task: mine the coveted Roses of the Night and escape alive. In Cordura, your sanity is your most scarce resource, and your own voice can betray you. As darkness deepens and paranoia sets in, a sinister entity known as "The Night" listens to your proximity voice chat, learns how you speak, and impersonates your teammates to lure you to your demise. Trust no one before paranoia makes you doubt everyone around you.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">4-Player Co-Op & Dynamic Scaling: Form up to 4-player squads to explore dark labyrinths, or brave the depths solo/in smaller teams with dynamic difficulty scaling procedural maps and entity aggression.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Voice-Mimicking & Deception System: Communicate using proximity voice chat, but beware: The Night hears you, calls you by name, and replicates the exact voices and visual appearances of your allies to deceive your squad.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Sanity Management & Companion Bond: As your sanity slips in the dark, the only way to restore it is to physically reunite with a teammate, if the person standing next to you is truly your friend and not a mimic.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Shifting Procedural Labyrinths: No two runs are the same; corrupt Victorian mansions reshape their corridors in real-time, forcing players to mark walls to keep their bearings before routes disappear.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Permanent Death & Customization: Recruit and customize your workers' physical appearances. Every misstep carries permanent death, requiring you to bring in new recruits to fill lost slots.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Extraction Objective Gameplay: Enter the ruins to mine the rare neurostimulant "Roses of the Night" (Ambrosia) and safely make it back to extraction before losing your mind.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming in 2027 to PC (Steam) and PlayStation 5 as a console exclusive.</p>
