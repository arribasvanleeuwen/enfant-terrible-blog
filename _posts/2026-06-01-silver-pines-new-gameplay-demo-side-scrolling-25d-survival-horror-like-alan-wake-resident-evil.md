<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Silver Pines is an upcoming 2.5D survival horror metroidvania following a troubled investigator through a surreal, reality-bending nightmare. Heavily inspired by Alan Wake and the classic Resident Evil, it blends psychological dread with intense resource management and side-scrolling exploration.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Silver Pines is an atmospheric survival horror game with a distinct side-scrolling metroidvania structure. It tells the dark story of Red Walker, a private investigator who arrives in the half-abandoned and half-forgotten town of Silver Pines to track down Eddie Velvet, a vanished musician. As the case begins to reveal a dark past, Walker slips into a strange and nightmarish descent beyond his control, forcing players to explore an interconnected world filled with dread, shadows, and secrets waiting to be revealed.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Classic Survival Horror Management: A love letter to classic survival horror, demanding careful management of strictly limited inventory space where every bullet, bottle of medicine, and upgrade matters to survive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Metroidvania Navigation and Exploration: Features a side-scrolling map filled with locked doors, hidden paths, and uncovered clues. Acquiring better tools and weapons grants access to more and more of the town.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visceral and Grounded Action: Teems with a large variety of challenging monsters and hulking bosses, making every single encounter with the horrors of the town tense and dangerous.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Investigative Puzzle Solving: Combines solving environmental puzzles blocking your way with unravelling a rich, deep mystery that could hold the ultimate key to redemption.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 8, 2026 to PS5, PC, Xbox and Switch.</p>
