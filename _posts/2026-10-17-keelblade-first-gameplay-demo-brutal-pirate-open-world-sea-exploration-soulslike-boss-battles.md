<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Exclusive preview of Keelblade, an upcoming fantasy pirate action RPG blending soulslite combat with 1-6 player co-op island exploration and customizable ship building.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The story of Keelblade revolves around the ultimate aspiration of every sailor: earning the legendary title of "Keelblade," a name feared across the islands and remembered long after their sails disappear. Your adventure begins on your own customizable pirate ship, which serves as your floating home and mobile base. You must set sail across uncharted waters to explore dangerous islands, gather hidden treasures and resources, craft powerful gear, and defeat colossal bosses ruling the seas to forge your pirate legend.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Soulslite Melee Combat & Magic: Engaging real-time combat that rewards precise timing, dodging, and parrying. Combine melee weapon setups (including dual-wielding) with flexible talent trees and powerful magic abilities.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Customizable Sailing Ship & Base: Build directly on the deck of your ship. Transform your vessel into a mobile workshop, kitchen, storage space, and command center as your journey and crew grow.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">1–6 Player Co-Op: Play solo or assemble an online crew with tetherless exploration. Experience shared quest progression with no disconnecting or summoning restrictions between areas and boss fights.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Island Exploration & Crafting: Sail to diverse, trap-filled islands to discover secrets, loot resources, and bring materials back home to research new crafting recipes and equipment upgrades.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Boss Raids & Challenge Modes: Track down and conquer fearsome regional bosses that test your team synergy. Replay defeated bosses under intensified conditions via dedicated high-difficulty Challenge Modes.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Planned for full release in 2027 on PC.</p>
