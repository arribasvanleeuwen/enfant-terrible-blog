<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set during the collapsing late Ming Dynasty, A Whisper of Fall: Jinyiwei immerses players in a narrative filled with political intrigue and shadowy factions. You play as an ordinary boatman forced to infiltrate the Jinyiwei (Imperial Guards) as an undercover agent for the black market. Grounded in stealth, tactical martial arts, and timeline-manipulation abilities, the game challenges you to solve cases, foresee future threats, and navigate the web of conspiracy to decide your ultimate fate.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Next-Gen Wuxia World: Built from the ground up in Unreal Engine 5 to deliver detailed historical environments, atmospheric dynamic lighting, and cinematic martial arts action.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unique Deduction Ability: Master a time-based deduction system across sandbox levels to move through shifting timelines, retrace scenarios, foresee potential threats, and alter outcomes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Traditional Wuxia Traversal & Stealth: Vault over walls, sprint across rooftops using lightness skills, and execute quiet stealth maneuvers to infiltrate enemy territory.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Martial Arts Combat: Engage in fast-paced duels with precise blocks, move counters, and authentic wuxia swordplay.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Investigative Sandbox Gameplay: Solve cases as a Jinyiwei, interrogate suspects, discover clues, and expose the conspirators operating within the empire.</p>
