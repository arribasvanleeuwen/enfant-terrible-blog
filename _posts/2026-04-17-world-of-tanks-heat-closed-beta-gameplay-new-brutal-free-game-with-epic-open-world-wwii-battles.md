<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">World of Tanks: HEAT is the first ever hero-driven tank action game. It's high-velocity clashes every single battle. It's Agents with unique abilities operating machines with menacing power. Choose your Agent, personalize your tank, and combine skill and strategy to dominate the battlefield.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 CLOSED BETA FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Eight Playable Agents with 3 roles: Defender, Assault, and Marksman.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Starter Roster: Everyone begins with Chopper (Defender), Kent (Assault), and Hound (Marksman).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unique Kits: Each Agent features a distinct set of skills, a powerful Ultimate ability, and a specific combat function.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fixed Partnerships: Every Agent is paired with specific vehicles (e.g., Chopper pilots the M1E1).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Flexibility: As you progress, you can unlock a second vehicle option for an Agent to adapt to different tactical needs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Environments: Maps are based on real-world locations re-imagined with massive structures and alternate-history themes (e.g., aircraft carriers and canyon dams).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Game Modes: A Quick Match queue featuring four different objective-based modes, all of which include respawns.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Squad Play: Support for grouping with friends before entering the matchmaking queue.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Full Cross-Play: A unified player pool across PC (Steam/Wargaming Game Center), Steam Deck, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
