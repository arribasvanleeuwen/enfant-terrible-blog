<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">God Save Birmingham is an upcoming physics-driven zombie survival simulator set in a gritty, realistic medieval England. This Unreal Engine 5 gameplay showcases a brutal, hardcore world where players must master essential survival skills to endure a relentless undead siege.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">God Save Birmingham drops you into an apocalyptic reimagined 14th-century English market town as its lone survivor. The world has ended, and your former friends and neighbors are now a horde of ravenous undead. To survive this historical nightmare, you must scramble for basic necessities, manage your physical and mental health, and navigate a world where the laws of physics are just as dangerous as the zombies.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Closed Beta Sandbox: Experience a solo survival sandbox featuring a tutorial priory area and parts of the village, including the historic Moor Street and St. Martin's Church.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Physics-Driven Combat & Environment: Use traditional weapons like axes and spears, or get creative by throwing and kicking everyday objects like buckets, chairs, and ladders to trip up the horde. Be careful, tripping over ledges or falling can cause serious injury, like broken legs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hardcore Survival Mechanics: Manage dynamic weather, indoor/outdoor temperatures, injuries, and illnesses caused by spoiled food, dirty water, or zombie juice soakage. You must also maintain weapon durability and sharpness to ensure they don't break in combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Crafting, Cooking & Barricading: Level up your carpentry, tailoring, and blacksmithing to craft full armor sets. Cook hearty meals like pottage to stay fed, and fortify your chosen shelter by installing door latches, building fences, and placing barricades.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming soon in Early Access to PC (Steam). Early Access will feature co-op multiplayer. PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S planned for the full release.</p>
