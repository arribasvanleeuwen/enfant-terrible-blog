<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This exclusive preview of Terrible Lizards showcases a thrilling single player first person horror platformer that combines the prehistoric dread of Jurassic Park: Survival with the high-velocity parkour of Dying Light.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Something is very wrong at Dalton's Dinos, the once must-see Route 66 roadside attraction baking under the New Mexico sun. The staff have vanished, but a mysterious voice on a landline telephone begs you to search for a missing child. You'd leave... if your car hadn't died miles from anywhere. It's 1992, no cell phones, no rideshares, and no help coming. As you descend into abandoned dig sites, forgotten caverns, and monster-filled tunnels, you'll soon realize you are not alone and something dark is hunting you.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Horror Meets First Person Parkour: Blends atmospheric survival horror with momentum-driven first person movement. Outrun, jump, climb, wall-run, swing, goo bounce, and slide through dangerous environments to stay alive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">No Weapons, Pure Evasion: Combat is not an option. There are no weapons to fight back, forcing you to rely entirely on your movement speed, reflexes, and environmental awareness to outwit relentlessly pursuing creatures.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">A Mystery Buried: Explore crumbling 1990s roadside exhibits and subterranean research caverns to piece together what Dalton's Dinos was really hiding before everything went wrong.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Environmental Storytelling: Collect scattered cassette tapes, notes, and log recordings throughout the park to uncover the dark history of the attraction and its questionable scientific experiments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Intuitive Controls with High Mastery: Traversal feels immediate and accessible to pick up, while offering deep platforming precision for mastering high-speed escape chases and complex obstacles.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 29, 2026 to PS5, XBOX and PC.</p>
