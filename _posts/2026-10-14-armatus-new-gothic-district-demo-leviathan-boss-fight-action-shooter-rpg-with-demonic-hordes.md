<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Exclusive preview of Armatus, featuring hands-on combat through a new exclusive biome called Gothic District, concluding with a new intense boss encounter against Leviathan I.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in a gothic, post-apocalyptic Paris following a cataclysmic event known as "The Vanishing," you play as an immortal masked warrior protecting the last human survivors hidden in cathedral ruins. Tasked by four surviving clerics, you must carve a path through demon-infested hellscapes along "The Scoured Path" to locate the Sunless Gate to heaven.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hyperkinetic Hybrid Combat: Seamlessly chain modern firearms (SMGs, shotguns, assault rifles) with a signature melee scythe and supernatural celestial powers like holy fire or time manipulation. We loved the game's responsive gunplay and fluid movement mechanics.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">New Gothic District: Navigate ruined Parisian urban streets and vertical, dark gothic architecture where environmental hazards and aggressive demonic hordes test your movement and positioning. We were thoroughly impressed by the striking Unreal Engine 5 visual fidelity across these detailed environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">New Leviathan Boss: Battle colossal demonic horrors in multi-phase encounters, from the towering Monument to the deadly Leviathan, demanding precise dodges, weapon swapping, and strategic cooldown management. We found high replay value driven by these intense boss fights.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Scoured Path & Roguelite Loop: Traverse procedurally generated paths each run. While death resets temporary power-ups, permanent currencies and knowledge persist to unlock upgraded abilities for future attempts.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Synergistic Buildcrafting: Start each run heavily armed and hunt for game-breaking power combinations. Stack elemental attachments and celestial traits to melt boss health bars.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming January 7, 2027 to PC (Steam, Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S (Day One on Xbox Game Pass), and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
