<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This hands-on preview showcases Rayman Legends Retold, featuring a brand new playable stage that highlights the game's enhanced visual engine, expanded character roster, and chaotic co-op platforming action.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Rebuilt from the ground up, Rayman Legends Retold revisits the iconic magical universe of the Glade of Dreams. Players join Rayman, Globox, and Barbara as they run, jump, and punch their way through reimagined mythical worlds, enchanted painting portals, and legendary boss battles.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Modernized 2D Engine: Beautifully updated hand-drawn visuals rendered at high framerates with dynamic lighting and enhanced particle effects. While the original UbiArt Framework still holds up today, the extra lighting depth and silky-smooth performance make the world feel noticeably more vibrant on modern displays.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Remixed Musical Stages: Reimagined rhythm-based levels featuring expanded tracks and updated timing challenges synced to the beat. These fresh remixes introduce new rhythmic patterns that will throw off even veteran players who memorized the original runs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Seamless Co-Op Platforming: Supports up to 4-player drop-in/drop-out local and online multiplayer across all campaign missions. Multi-player integration feels effortless, making the addition of full online co-op a massive win alongside the classic chaos of couch co-op.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanded "Back to Origins" Content: Remastered classic levels alongside brand-new secret stages, hidden collectibles, and tough invasion challenges. The new secret stages offer a welcome spike in difficulty, while the sheer volume of content never feels like simple filler.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Refined Character Mechanics: Streamlined assist mechanics seamlessly adapted for modern controllers, alongside custom skin unlocks. Control remapping feels great across the board, especially translating old touch-screen Murfy interactions into intuitive button prompts, making single player runs far less clunky.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 1, 2026 to PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
