<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Grounded in the logic of the SAW franchise and set in the aftermath of WWI, SAW: Genesis challenges players to either test their targets' will to live as the Judge or survive brutal trials through strategy, teamwork, and impossible moral decisions as the Accused.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">3v1 Asymmetric Multiplayer Horror: Pits three Accused against a single Judge in intense, high-stakes matches.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Procedurally Generated Maps: Features dynamic maps that combine deadly traps, tactical positioning, and unpredictable trial environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Morally Charged Mechanics: Blends survival, teamwork, and impossible moral choices as players navigate the Judge's unnerving trials.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual Playable Roles: Allows players to step into the role of the Judge delivering trials or the Accused striving to survive them.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Lore Elements: Incorporates franchise-inspired lore and characters like Bette the Puppet, the Doctor, and the Old Hand.</p>
