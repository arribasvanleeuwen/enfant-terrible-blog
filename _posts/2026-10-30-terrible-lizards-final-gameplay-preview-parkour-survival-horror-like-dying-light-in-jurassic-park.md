<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This exclusive RTX 5090 preview of Terrible Lizards showcases a horror survival platformer that combines the prehistoric atmosphere of Jurassic Park with the brutal parkour of Dying Light.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Terrible Lizards is a horror parkour game set against the backdrop of an old Route 66 attraction with something dark and sinister lurking below. To escape, you’ll need to move fast, navigate challenging terrain, and uncover what Dalton’s Dinos is really hiding. You’d better start running.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">First Person Parkour Horror: Blends atmospheric survival horror with fast first person movement. Run, jump, climb, wall-run, swing, and slide through dangerous environments to stay alive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">No Weapons: There are no weapons to fight back, forcing you to rely entirely on your movement speed to escape from the brutal dino creatures.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Puzzle Exploration: Investigate crumbling 1990s roadside exhibits and subterranean research caverns to piece together what Dalton's Dinos is hiding. Collect cassette tapes, notes, and log recordings throughout the park to uncover the dark history of the attraction and its questionable scientific experiments.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 29, 2026 to PS5, XBOX and PC.</p>
