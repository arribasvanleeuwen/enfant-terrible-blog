<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Get an exclusive look at Marvel's Wolverine, an upcoming action adventure game built on Insomniac's proprietary engine. This preview showcases the game's non-linear hub exploration, Team X missions, and unique brutal combat mechanics.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You play as James "Logan" Howlett (Wolverine) in an original story where mutants face the threat of annihilation. Logan teams up with key allies like Mystique and Sabretooth while confronting anti-mutant operations led by Bolivar Trask.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Big Linear Hubs and Exploration: We got to explore big environments, specifically Lowtown in Madripoor and the iconic Princess Bar. The world balances tight linear action with open hubs where you can take a breather, converse with local mutants, interact with the environment, and use Logan's Enhanced Senses to track distinct scent trails through the streets to uncover hidden items.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fast Parry-Driven Combat: Playing through the combat sections immediately shows a heavy focus on momentum and aggressive parrying. Logan closes distances rapidly with high-velocity leaps, chaining claw attacks into heavy directional kicks that break enemy guards and send foes crashing into walls or off ledges.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Environmental Kills and Dynamic Team-Ups: The battlefields are highly interactive, allowing us to slam enemies into heavy machinery like forklifts or trigger brutal contextual executions. During story encounters, combat seamlessly incorporates team-up attacks, letting you coordinate strikes alongside allies like Mystique and Sabretooth.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">3-Tier Rage Meter: Building combos fills Logan's Rage Meter through three distinct tiers, where reaching Tier 2 unlocks the Last Stand ability to instantly revive Logan from defeat by consuming accumulated rage, while reaching Tier 3 triggers a primal berserker state that enhances overall damage output and enables high-impact execution strikes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Enhanced Senses, Stealth, and Inhibitors: Stepping into stealth scenarios, activating Logan's Enhanced Senses highlights enemy heartbeats and movement paths, setting up lethal drop-takedowns from high perches. However, Trask's soldiers introduce Ability Inhibitors that suppress your healing factor, completely changing the tempo and forcing you to destroy the devices immediately.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Boss Encounters: The hands-on session wrapped up with an intense multi-phase boss fight against a Sentinel Prototype. Defeating it requires precise timing to parry heavy attacks, dodging massive environmental hazards, and using your team's unique abilities to dismantle the unit piece by piece.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming September 15, 2026 exclusively to PlayStation 5.</p>
