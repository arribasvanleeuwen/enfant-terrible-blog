<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Breathedge 2 is a story-driven adventure set on the distant fringes of inhabited space. Players venture through shipwrecks and ruined settlements, using blueprints and tools to dismantle machinery, gather resources, and upgrade gear. Alongside an unruly crew, you will meet new characters and gradually untangle the powerful forces carving up the galaxy.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Garbage-to-Gadget Salvaging: Scavenge abandoned stations and shipwrecks to take everything not glued down, turning salvaged junk into vital survival tools and gear.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Crafting & Equipment Upgrades: Collect blueprints, repair or dismantle machinery, and upgrade your gear to unlock new paths and reach previously inaccessible areas.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Environmental Survival: Navigate open space while closely managing life-support systems, including oxygen supply, extreme temperatures, and harsh environmental hazards.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Story-Driven Galaxy Exploration: Follow the Man and his crew as you meet new characters, uncover lore on the fringes of space, and expose the factions vying for control of the galaxy.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Early Access: The game features a strong balance of humor and open world scavenging. The Early Access release features the first major story chapter, a massive region to explore, and core crafting systems ready for future updates.</p>
