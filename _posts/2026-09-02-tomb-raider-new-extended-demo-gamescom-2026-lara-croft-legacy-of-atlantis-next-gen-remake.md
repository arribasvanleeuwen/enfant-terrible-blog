<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis follows archaeologist Lara Croft as she is hired by Natla Technologies to recover pieces of the ancient Scion artifact.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Revamped St. Francis' Folly & Greece: The game reimagines iconic Mediterranean ruins with dramatic verticality and complex environmental puzzles. The scale and atmospheric lighting powered by Unreal Engine 5 make navigating towering columns and sheer drop-offs feel far more organic, tactile, and imposing than the classic 1996 grid layout.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Modernized Traversal & Grappling Hook: The remake introduces an expanded movement kit featuring a grappling hook for wall-running, dynamic swing points, and anchored lines to ascend or descend freely. These dynamic tools elevate exploration while perfectly retaining Lara's signature acrobatic flourishes and platforming precision.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Redesigned Sword of Damocles Room: Completely overhauls the infamous single-hit trap room into a tense, multi-layered mechanical challenge. The clearer visual cues, tight dodge windows, and ability to shoot down falling blades eliminate frustrating, unfair deaths without losing an ounce of heart-pounding tension.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Acrobatic Combat & The Focus System: Fuses fast-paced dual-pistol gunplay with acrobatic dodging and a time-slowing Focus mechanic for pinpoint targeting. Engaging swarms of bats and aggressive panthers feels undeniably cinematic while still demanding smart positioning and quick instincts.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Optional Scanner & Tactical Skill Tree: Features a togglable environment scanner for ancient lore or puzzle hints, alongside a versatile skill tree to upgrade Lara's abilities, such as the "Deadeye" skill used to disarm distant room mechanics. It strikes a great balance, letting you tailor the experience between raw, unguided raiding and modern convenience.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Resource Crafting & Relic Economy: Gather environmental materials to craft healing items and protective buffs on the go, while unearthing historical Relics scattered across the ruins. Unlocking iconic cosmetics like Lara's original 1996 retro outfit through relic hunting adds a brilliant layer of rewarding nostalgia.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming February 12, 2027 (Deluxe Edition offers 48-hour early access) to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam), and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
