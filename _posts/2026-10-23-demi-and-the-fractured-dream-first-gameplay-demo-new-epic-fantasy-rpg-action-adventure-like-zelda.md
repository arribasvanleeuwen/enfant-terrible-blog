<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Experience a first look at Demi and the Fractured Dream, an upcoming Unity 6-powered action adventure puzzle game reminiscent of The Legend of Zelda, where players harness void powers to save a mysterious world from legendary threats.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Demi and the Fractured Dream is an action adventure game set in Somnus, a captivating realm threatened by the Accursed Beasts of legend. Play as Demi, a Voidsent born into this world who can harness the powers of the void, a force that grants immense power while slowly consuming him. Guided by a mysterious voice, players must navigate diverse lands, defeat monster threats, solve complex puzzles, and uncover the truth behind an ancient history and Demi's own role in the world.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Voidsent Powers & Arsenal: Combine a wide arsenal of attacks, tools, and spells to vanquish void monsters and enemies using classic action adventure combat mechanics.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Character Progression & Charms: Unlock Demi's full potential by equipping a wide range of ability-enhancing charms to adapt your character build to your preferred playstyle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Environmental Puzzles & Exploration: Traverse atmospheric realms and tackle dungeons filled with complex environmental puzzles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Platforming: Engage in dynamic platforming to reach treacherous heights and navigate the vertical landscape of Somnus.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Releasing in February 2027 for PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
