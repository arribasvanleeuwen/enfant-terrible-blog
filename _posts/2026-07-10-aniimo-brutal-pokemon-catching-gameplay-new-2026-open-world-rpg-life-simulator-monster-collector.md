<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Aniimo is a free-to-play, next-gen creature-catching open world RPG. Explore the vast continent of Idyll, Twine with unique Aniimo, unlock new abilities. Travel to a sky-floating metropolis, meet other Pathfinders, and battle in exciting competitions. Choose your Aniimo and begin your epic journey!</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Unique "Twine" System: Merge and transform with your captured Aniimo to gain their traversal abilities (like gliding or digging) and explore the vibrant open world through their eyes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Real-Time ARPG Combat & Collection: Battle and capture over 200 magical, elemental creatures. Create a party of up to 4 Aniimo and swap between them in fast-paced, real-time action RPG combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Brand New Region & World Bosses: This closed beta introduces the "Island of Peace," an aquatic-themed zone packed with new puzzles, unique wild creatures, and massive PvE co-op bosses like Bubbask and Coraliz.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanded Customization & Trading: Personalize your moving RV base with new furniture and shops, and utilize the newly added player-to-player Aniimo Trading system to complete your collection.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">New 3v3v3v3v3v3 PvP Mode: Test your skills in the brand-new "Diamond Egg Heist," a chaotic 6-team competitive mode where you must secure and escort a precious egg across the map.</p>
