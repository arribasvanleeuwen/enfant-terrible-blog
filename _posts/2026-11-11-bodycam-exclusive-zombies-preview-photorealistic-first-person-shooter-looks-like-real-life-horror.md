<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Bodycam is an ultra realistic multiplayer first person shooter powered by Unreal Engine 5. The v0.9 "The Dead & Damned" update puts the spotlight back on cooperative survival horror for up to 4 players.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Reworked Mode & Maps: Zombies returns with improved spawns, zombie behavior, and a full polish pass on both the Village and Asylum maps for up to 4-player co-op.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Zombie Trenches: Experience a brand new Zombie game mode built directly on the Trenches map.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Upgraded Weapon Boxes: Weapon boxes in Zombies now drop firearms equipped with random attachments using the game's loadout attachment system for greater variety on every run.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">New Loadout Weapons: The Crossbow (previously only accessible via the Village secret) is now available directly in your loadout for PvP maps, joined by the TEC-9, the game's first secondary machine pistol.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Reworked Public Pool Map: Public Pool receives a major visual rework and layout improvements to match Bodycam's latest quality standards.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Bodycam v0.9 The Dead & Damned launches on November 13, 2026, for PC (Steam).</p>
