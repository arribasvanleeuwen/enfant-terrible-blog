Agefield High: Rock the School is an open world coming-of-age action adventure game (made by a small team, Refugium Games, creators of Greyhill Incident) set in the early 2000s that plunges players into high school rebellion, combining a time-based schedule, straightforward combat, and dynamic activities. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in the early 2000s, Agefield High: Rock the School follows Sam, a teenager uprooted by his parents and dropped into Agefield High just months before graduation. On day one, Sam gets pulled into a scheme with his new friends Kale and Axel to pull off the ultimate "Rock the School" stunt and leave their mark as legends. Balance class attendance, earn pocket money, avoid teachers and campus security, and explore a nostalgic small town packed with early 2000s fashion, music, and pop culture.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Time & School System: Attend daily classes like English, Math, Geography, German, and Music to earn cash legally, or skip out to pull off side jobs while avoiding watchful teachers and police.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Branching Story & Missions: Experience 32 main story missions across 8–10 hours of gameplay with multiple choices leading to two distinct endings.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Handcrafted Open World: Explore a compact town featuring the high school campus, two residential neighborhoods, a town center, and countryside filled with 15 side missions.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Town Customization & Shops: Spend earned pocket money at local shops on various bicycles (BMX, racing, country), outfits, haircuts, tattoos, and nostalgic accessories.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Combat & Vehicles: Engage in simple, old-school combat mechanics and navigate the town using bikes while keeping a low profile.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Early 2000s Teen Comedy Vibe: Relive an unapologetic era of pop culture inspired by classic edgy teen rebellion movies and pop-punk music aesthetics.</p>
