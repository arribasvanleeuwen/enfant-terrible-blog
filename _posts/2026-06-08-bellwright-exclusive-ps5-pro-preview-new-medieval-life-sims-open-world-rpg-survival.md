<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Bellwright is an open world RPG set in medieval times and can be played solo or co-op. Framed for a prince’s murder, you return from exile after surviving an assassination attempt, uncover a deeper conspiracy, and lead a rebellion against the Crown. Build settlements, recruit allies, and command forces to reclaim your homeland and reveal your family’s secrets while learning more about a life you had to leave behind. Command your forces, prove your valor through combat, and cement yourself as the hero of your people.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Settlement Building: Design, build, and expand your own medieval villages with structures for crafting, housing, and defense.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Villager Recruitment and Management: Recruit NPCs, assign them jobs, and automate tasks like farming, crafting, and resource gathering.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Territory Liberation: Take control of regions from the Crown, free settlements, and expand your influence across the map.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Crafting and Resource Systems: Gather materials, craft weapons, tools, and buildings, and manage supply chains to sustain growth.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Combat and Army Command: Engage in real-time combat and lead squads of recruits into battles against enemy forces.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Narrative Progression: Uncover a story-driven conspiracy while completing quests and learning about your past.</p>
