<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You play as an ordinary citizen who has been unexpectedly accused of a crime you didn't commit. Trapped in a provincial Soviet town watched over by a towering giant policeman, you must plan your escape using any means necessary, whether by sneaking, trading, deceiving, or even charming the giant officer overseeing your every move.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Kafkaesque Escape Simulator: Explore a surreal, Soviet-inspired town and find creative ways to flee while under constant scrutiny.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Colossal Policeman AI: Navigate around a gigantic authority figure who watches your actions, remembers past attempts, and dynamically changes his behavior and mood.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Reactive Mood & Dialogue System: Interact with townspeople and the giant policeman using voice/text dialogue, gifts, and persuasion, shifting their emotions to gain assistance or distractions.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Emergent Immersive Sim Sandbox: Choose your own path to freedom through stealing, trading, trespassing, or bribing, with multiple escape routes such as trains or cable cars.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Town & Schedules: Inhabitants and environments function on independent daily routines, offering strategic timing windows for stealth and exploration.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Consequences & Replayability: Every escape attempt adapts to your previous actions, leaving lasting consequences on the town and its citizens.</p>
