<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The story of Rustmourne begins as you wake up inside an ancient, derelict megastructure trapped in an endless cycle of decay and renewal. Hunted by horrific mutated beings that stalk the dark corridors, you must uncover the mystery of your awakening and find a way out alive. Guided by a haunting voice in your head that refuses to let you stay dead, you must use weapons, stealth, and mysterious powers to survive terrifying encounters while paying the dark price of resurrection.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Dismemberment: Amputate specific enemy limbs using firearms to slow, blind, or cripple mutated creatures, dynamically altering their behavior and threats.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Immersive VR Weapon Physics & Handling: Experience tactile interaction with custom-designed sci-fi firearms, complete with manual reloading, environmental mechanics, and physical interaction.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stealth & Environmental Sound Tactics: Use ambient sounds and distraction tactics to bypass crawling abominations or manipulate enemy paths through the megastructure.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Scarcity, Powers & Creative Combat: Combine scarce ammunition with unique supernatural abilities and environmental interactions to turn the tide in deadly combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unique Resurrection Loop: Dying is not the end, as a mysterious internal voice revives you, but returning to life comes at a cost that changes your journey.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming Early 2027 to PC VR (SteamVR for Valve Index and Meta Quest family headsets).</p>
