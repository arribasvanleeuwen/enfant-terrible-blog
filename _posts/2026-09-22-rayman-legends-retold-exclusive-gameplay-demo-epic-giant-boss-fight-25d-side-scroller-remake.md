<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This exclusive hands-on preview showcases the remake Rayman Legends Retold, featuring a brand new playable musical stages like Macarena, new exciting dragon-riding levels, and a fresh look of the Land of the Living Dead.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Rebuilt from the ground up, Rayman Legends Retold revisits the iconic magical universe of the Glade of Dreams. Players join Rayman, Globox, and Barbara as they run, jump, and punch their way through reimagined mythical worlds, enchanted painting portals, and legendary boss battles.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Modernized 3D Engine: Features a complete transition from classic 2D hand-drawn artwork to a fully rendered 3D visual style. While swapping out the hand-drawn look trades away a bit of the original 2D charm, the graphical refinement looks stunning, particularly in the spy-thriller world inspired by Splinter Cell and James Bond films, which looks sleek and remarkably vibrant on modern displays.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Reimagined Musical & Flying Stages: Delivers hands-on access to fresh content, including rhythmically updated musical stages and high-flying dragon riding levels. Hovering through the air and dashing through beat-synced tracks showcase just how well the core design holds up while offering an incredible amount of momentum.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">World-Specific Gameplay Variety: Adapts mechanics dynamically based on the current world, introducing drastic shifts in how you navigate each section. From stealthy spy homage levels to dragon-fueled aerial action, this design keeps the moment-to-moment gameplay exceptionally fresh.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tight Physics & Masterful Level Design: Preserves the pixel-perfect controls, dynamic physics, and inventive stage setups that defined the original release. The core movement remains deeply satisfying, hitting the exact sweet spot fans expect from top-tier platformers like Super Mario or Donkey Kong.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Camera Angles: Integrates cinematic, shifting camera perspectives that add visual flair to high-speed platforming runs. However, these new 3D angles can occasionally make it difficult to read the full stage layout during fast-paced sequences, requiring sharper reflexes from players.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Amazing Soundtrack: The game's music has been expanded with 55 minutes of brand new music. Original composer Christophe Héral returned to compose the score alongside Grant Kirkhope, famous for his musical work on the Banjo-Kazooie and Mario + Rabbids series.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Skins: Most of the skins from the original game will return alongside a few from Rayman Adventures and new ones. The majority of the skins will be unlocked through a new system in the Tree of Tranquility and many heroes are confirmed to return.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming December 3, 2026 to PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
