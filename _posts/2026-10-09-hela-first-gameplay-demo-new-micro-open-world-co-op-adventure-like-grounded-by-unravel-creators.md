<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Exclusive RTX 5090 gameplay preview of Hela: of Mice & Magic, an upcoming 3D open world co-op adventure that combines Scandinavian natural landscapes with exploration and heartwarming puzzle mechanics from the creators of Unravel.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You control a brave little mouse serving a kind witch who has long protected the forest. When she suddenly falls ill, you and your mouse companions must step outside the safety of home to gather ingredients, craft magical potions, and perform acts of kindness throughout the land to restore harmony and save her.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Heartwarming Open World Adventure: Venture through a sprawling, handcrafted Scandinavian landscape inspired by the rugged mountains, misty wetlands, and tranquil lakes of Northern Sweden.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-Op & Solo Flexibility: Play through the full story alone, in local split-screen co-op, or with up to 4 players online to overcome obstacles together.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Shade System: When playing solo, summon magical copies of yourself to assist with multi-mouse puzzles and world interactions seamlessly.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Micro-Perspective Traversal: See everyday human objects from a mouse’s point of view, using physics-based mechanics, climbing abilities, gliders, and magical backpacks to navigate giant obstacles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Potion Brewing & Healing: Scavenge the wild for ingredients, learn potion recipes, and deliver remedies to help forest creatures and nearby communities in need.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Environmental Puzzle-Solving: Work collaboratively or utilize magical familiars to interact with dynamic puzzles scattered across open environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Wholesome Narrative & Immersion: Experience a cozy, comfort-focused storyline centering on kindness, caretaking, and curiosity without high-stress combat mechanics.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming December 1, 2026 to PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S (Day One on Xbox Game Pass), and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
