<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You play as Fawkes, a prisoner sentenced to explore the depths of the Abyss. Throughout your journey, you are monitored by an artificial warden named Aylin. You'll uncover the secrets of the lost city of Greymont and a mysterious prophecy.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Brutalist Sci-Fi Setting: You explore the "Mimic Moon" Luna, a derelict megastructure filled with ancient ruins and abandoned technology. The architecture is massive and imposing, drawing heavy inspiration from the brutalist style.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Bullet Hell: Unlike traditional shooters, combat involves dodging complex patterns of projectiles, similar to games like Returnal.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fluid Platforming: Movement is key; you will dash, jump, and sprint to navigate vertical environments and survive relentless enemy encounters.</p>
