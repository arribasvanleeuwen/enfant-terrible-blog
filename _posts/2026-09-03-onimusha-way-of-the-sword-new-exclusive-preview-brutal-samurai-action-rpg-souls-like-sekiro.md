<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in a corrupted, demon-infested vision of early Edo-period Kyoto, Onimusha: Way of the Sword follows the young samurai Miyamoto Musashi after acquiring an Oni Gauntlet. Built around precise timing and defensive positioning, combat requires mastering deflects and counter-attacks to break enemy stamina gauges. As players progress through cursed temples and surrounding areas like Kiyomizu-dera, they utilize the Oni Gauntlet to absorb defeated souls, upgrade their abilities, and take on formidable Genma threats.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Precision-Driven Sword Combat: Centered around timed parries, evasive dodges, and posture mechanics. The fluid combat loop offers immense satisfaction with perfectly timed counters, drawing strong comparisons to Sekiro's intense deflection balance and marking the triumphant return of Capcom's classic Issen technique.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Return of the Oni Gauntlet: Slay Genma foes to absorb red souls in real-time, allowing Musashi to unlock martial skills, enhance weapon stance trees, and unleash mystical abilities in battle. Soul absorption seamlessly feeds directly back into active combat progression during early-game encounters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visuals Powered by RE Engine: Delivers highly detailed character models, fluid sword clash animations, and haunting lighting effects across dark Japanese landscapes. Highly expressive animations and stunning visual polish bring incredible atmospheric depth to blood-soaked bamboo groves and burning shrine gates.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Demonic Boss Battles: Features intense skill-check encounters against eccentric swordsmen and grotesque beasts. These complex, multi-stage boss fights stand out as a major highlight of the opening hours, successfully channeling the punishing yet rewarding encounter design of Nioh.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Semi-Open Structure & Hubs: Blends linear narrative missions with explorable hub regions, allowing players to discover secrets, undertake side tasks, and gather upgrade resources. While early pacing relies on tutorial-heavy progression, the underlying exploration and mechanical depth offer a thoroughly engaging foundation.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming September 4, 2026 to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (via Steam).</p>
