<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Check out our exclusive demo of Neo Berlin 2087, an Unreal Engine 5 action thriller combining dark cyberpunk investigation with tactical combat. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in a dark, dystopian vision of Berlin in 2087, you play as Nolan, a troubled detective tasked with finding the Police Chief's daughter after a sudden murder. Navigating a city divided by technology and wealth, Nolan must brave both the luxurious walled metropolis and the dangerous, machine-plagued wastelands while uncovering a sinister conspiracy that threatens to consume the entire city.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mind-Bending Investigation: Step directly into the last moments of victims, reconstruct their memories, and decode human behavior to unlock crucial clues and solve cases.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual Perspective Combat & Stealth: Seamlessly switch between first and third person perspectives to silently eliminate threats or dive straight into action-packed gunfights and close-quarters combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic RPG Mechanics: Customize a wide array of futuristic weaponry and tailor your skill set to fit your preferred playstyle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Narrative Choice & Consequences: Steer complex conversations, navigate political landscapes, and let your moral choices directly shape the story and the characters around you.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visually Stunning Dystopia: Powered by Unreal Engine 5 to showcase the striking contrast between Neo Berlin's high-tech luxury and its brutal, robot-infested outer settlements.</p>
