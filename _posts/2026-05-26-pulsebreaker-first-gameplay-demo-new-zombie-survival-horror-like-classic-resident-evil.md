<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Developed by a single creator using Unity 6, Pulsebreaker is an upcoming classic survival horror game inspired by 90s hits like Resident Evil and Fear Effect. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Pulsebreaker is a classic survival horror game inspired by classics from the Playstation 1 Era. It blends tense exploration and puzzle solving with light action mechanics, forcing you to carefully manage resources, choose what to carry, and decide which paths to take. Every decision matters and a single mistake could mean a grisly end at the hands of zombies and other freakish creatures.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Atmospheric PS1-Style Horror: Inspired by classics from the PlayStation 1 era. The game features a beautifully crafted retro visual style with fixed camera angles that drop you into claustrophobic, dark, and unsettling environments filled with relentless threats.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Limited Inventory Management: Inventory space is scarce and every single slot matters. You are forced to make tough, tactical calls about what to carry and what to leave behind as you navigate tight corridors. Overpacking cripples your efficiency; underpreparing leaves you vulnerable.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Focus Meter: A combat system that rewards precision and composure under pressure. Once filled, it unlocks powerful abilities to turn the tide: you can chain multi-stagger combos, land critical headshots, and deliver well-timed melee kicks to keep enemies at bay.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Resource Management and Combat: Blends tense exploration with light action mechanics. You must fight your way through hordes of "pulses" (zombies and other undead creatures), forcing you to use your limited ammunition wisely.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Puzzles and Exploration: Progress requires exploring the eerie environments, collecting resources, managing supplies, and solving light environmental puzzles to open up new paths. Every decision matters, and a single mistake can lead to a grisly end.</p>
