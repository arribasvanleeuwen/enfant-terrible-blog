<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Valor Mortis is an upcoming first person action RPG that blends demanding souls-like combat with agile parkour traversal. Set in a dark, plague-ridden 19th-century landscape, players must use supernatural transmutation powers to survive brutal monstrosities.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Valor Mortis takes place in a corrupted 19th-century Europe scorched by endless war and a mysterious, dark plague. The story follows William, a former soldier of Napoleon’s Grande Armée who is brought back from a battlefield grave. Reborn with the very corrupting power of the plague pulsing through his veins, William awakens to a world he no longer recognizes. He must navigate a horrific landscape, put a stop to Napoleon’s undead Eternal Guard, and venture into the darkest corners of the continent to uncover the truth behind his unholy resurrection and the global conspiracy driving the madness.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">First Person Souls-like: Experience a highly demanding combat system from a first person perspective, featuring precise parries, swift dashes, and visceral finishers. Every death serves as an opportunity to grow stronger.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Parkour Traversal: Drawing from the developer's experience with Ghostrunner, players utilize aggressive movement mechanics such as grappling, wall-running, and swinging to explore Metroidvania-inspired environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The New Lighthouse Level: While the first demo level serves as a refined tutorial on a ravaged battlefield, the newly added second level tasks players with using transmutation powers to traverse the broken cobblestones of a destroyed bridge leading to a Lighthouse.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanded Arsenal and Transmutations: The second level introduces new equipment to diversify playstyles, including the Rapier, the Musket, and a unique shield transmutation capability to withstand tougher challenges and more aggressive enemies.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Supernatural Abilities: Players can channel their internal plague corruption to enhance physical attacks and unlock devastating supernatural abilities to inflict upon otherworldly monstrosities.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming September 24, 2026 to PS5, Xbox and PC.</p>
