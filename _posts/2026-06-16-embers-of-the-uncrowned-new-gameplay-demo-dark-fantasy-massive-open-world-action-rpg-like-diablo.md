<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Your quest unfolds in Embers of the Uncrowned, a dark fantasy MMORPG presented in a rich isometric view. In a continent ravaged by an elven invasion, you are the last hope for House Harborwell. Born illegitimate, you left home to become a mercenary, only to find that destiny has called you back. Now, as the rightful lord, you must reclaim your lost domain and rebuild your house. Fight back against the corrupted creatures and elven forces that plague the continent.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unique Classes & Skill Trees: Master distinct combat styles to dominate the battlefield. Choose your path as the Spectral Blade, carving through foes with swift longsword mastery; the Executioner, annihilating enemies with crushing giant axe blows; or the Stormbringer, calling down wind and lightning magic. Experiment with deep skill trees to tailor your attributes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Loyal Companion System: You never fight alone. Recruit trusted companions to stand by your side, level up together, and create powerful tactical synergies to bolster your forces against the elven invasion.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Colossal Co-op Threats & Raids: Band together with other players to conquer massive challenges. Defeat powerful Domain Bosses to reclaim your land and restore your House's honor, or enter high-stakes Raids where victory requires flawless cooperation and perfect execution.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Settlement Expansion & Domain Rebuilding: Your personal strength reflects your House's power. Liberate war-torn regions to grow a humble mercenary camp into a bustling, prosperous city as your glory and domain expand across the continent.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Infinite Progression & Hidden Stories: Push your character's potential to the absolute limit by combining Skill Gems, enhancing gear, and awakening the ancient power of your bloodline, all while exploring a world filled with tragic secrets.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Character Customization: Express your individuality in a dark fantasy world with detailed face and body customization, alongside a massive array of distinctive costumes to mix and match.</p>
