<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The game follows the journey of Macduff, a grizzled mercenary leader struggling to survive in the harsh world of Pywel. <p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Pywel is a land of shifting alliances and brutal power struggles between different factions and desert tribes.
