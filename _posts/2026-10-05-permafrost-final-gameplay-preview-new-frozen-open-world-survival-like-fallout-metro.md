  <div class="description-container"><h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">A single player and co-op open world survival game set in a perpetual winter, Permafrost combines brutal cold mechanics, base building, and hostile threats in an Unreal Engine 5 post-apocalyptic world inspired by Metro and Frostpunk.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">In Permafrost, players step into the frozen remnants of human civilization after a cataclysmic frost shatters the world. Taking on the role of a desperate survivor, you must navigate buried city ruins, scavenge scarce resources, and build shelters to protect yourself against the deadly sub-zero temperatures. Combining deep survival mechanics with strategic crafting and base building, players utilize specialized gear, restore broken technology, and fight off dangerous localized factions or wild beasts. Whether traveling alone with your loyal dog or teaming up in up to 4-player co-op, every decision means the difference between life and freezing to death.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unforgiving Cold Mechanics: Manage body temperature, hunger, and gear durability against freezing blizzards and extreme weather hazards.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Building & Shelter Networks: Scavenge materials to construct bases, repair abandoned structures, and establish safe strongholds across the open world.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Loyal Dog Companion: Rescue and travel with a canine companion that scouts for danger, carries extra supplies, and keeps you company in the silent apocalypse.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Open World Co-Op Survival: Play solo or group up with up to 3 other players to share resources, defend territories, and survive together.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 9, 2026 to PC in Early Access (via Steam, Epic Games Store, and GOG).</p>
