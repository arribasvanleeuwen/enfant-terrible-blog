<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This Unreal Engine 5 gameplay preview showcases Nakwon: Last Paradise, a brutal extraction survival game featuring stealthy scavenging and social management. It blends mechanics from titles like Escape from Tarkov and Project Zomboid to create a gritty, zombie-infested multiplayer experience.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Nakwon is a multiplayer zombie survival extraction game set in a devastated city overrun by the infected. Players begin as refugees living in a survivor shelter and must venture into the dangerous urban districts to search for food, materials and equipment needed to stay alive. Every expedition is a high-risk mission where zombies and other survivors compete for the same limited resources. Players must maintain their shelter, craft equipment and prepare for dangerous scavenging runs.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Extraction-Based Loop: Players must enter the city to gather resources and escape back to the "Safety Zone." If you die before extracting, you lose all the equipment and items you were carrying.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-op Gameplay: You can enter the Looting Zone in small squads (typically up to 2 or 3 players in standard extraction runs).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stealth-Centric Combat: Unlike traditional shooters, combat is extremely risky. Zombies are sensitive to sound and can easily overwhelm players. Sneaking, distracting enemies with bricks or bottles, and avoiding detection are prioritized over direct confrontation.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Day/Night Cycle Mechanics: The behavior of zombies changes based on the light. Nighttime increases visibility risks for humans while potentially altering zombie aggression or patterns.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Safety Zone Economy: Between runs, players reside in a simulated society with a strict hierarchy. You must earn currency to pay rent, buy better gear, and increase your Citizen Grade to unlock better living conditions and perks.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical PVE PVP: You aren't just fighting zombies; other players are also in the zone. You can choose to cooperate with strangers to survive or betray them to take their loot for yourself.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Immersive Urban Setting: The game features a highly detailed, realistic recreation of Seoul, focusing on narrow alleyways, multi-story buildings, and cramped interiors that create a claustrophobic atmosphere.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Limited Resources: Ammunition and high-tier weapons are scarce. Players often have to rely on improvised melee weapons and careful stamina management to survive encounters.</p>
