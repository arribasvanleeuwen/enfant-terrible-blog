<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">EXODUS is a single player third person sci-fi action RPG where players take on the role of a Traveler in a far-future universe. Navigating a hostile galaxy dominated by advanced alien species, players must fight for humanity's survival while grappling with the real-time consequences of interstellar travel.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Liberating the Kay mission: In this demo, you’re seeking aid from Suliman Tenjin, leader of the powerful Tenjin Dynasty. But his help comes at a price: a favor that leads straight into a fight against your half-brother Gideon Aslan’s forces for control of Khonsu, an orbital space station that’s protected your homeworld and been home to the Kay for generations. Along the way, you’ll face decisions both big and small that reflect your path toward becoming a Traveler, Paladin (virtue) or Immortal (ambition).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">BioWare Veterans: Developed by ex-BioWare creators, it features a third person cover shooter structure, companion ability management, character-driven narrative choices, and branching decision-making.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Arsenal & Gadgets: Includes a variety of high-tech weapons, offensive devices, and active abilities that allow for multiple combat strategies and playstyles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Companion Directives: A real-time squad command system that allows players to issue direct orders to squadmates, combining complementary combat abilities, stealth maneuvers, or direct engagement strategies.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Time Dilation Mechanics: Interstellar travel mechanics where time passes faster on target destinations relative to the home world. Decisions and missions impact characters, faction relationships, and civilizational development across multi-generational timeframes.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming on April 7, 2027 to PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
