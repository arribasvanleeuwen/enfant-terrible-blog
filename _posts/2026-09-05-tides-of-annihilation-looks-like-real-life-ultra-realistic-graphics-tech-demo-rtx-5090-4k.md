<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

After an otherworldly invasion shatters London, Gwendolyn, one of the few survivors, sets out on a dangerous journey to fight back against the invading forces and uncover the truth hidden beyond the veil.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 TECHNICAL FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Full Path Tracing Support: Officially confirmed to launch with full Path Tracing (Full Ray Tracing) on PC, handling real-time global illumination, dynamic bounces, accurate reflections, and ambient shadows.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">NVIDIA DLSS 4.5 Integration: Planned for launch day, featuring AI-driven Super Resolution, Frame Generation, and Ray Reconstruction to maintain smooth frame rates with demanding Path Tracing enabled.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">DLSS Ray Reconstruction: Utilizes NVIDIA's AI neural network to replace hand-tuned denoisers, improving temporal stability and lighting precision across complex geometry.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unreal Engine 5 Core: Developed using Unreal Engine 5 in close technical collaboration with Epic Games and NVIDIA for GPU-level optimization.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">4K Ultra Hardware Demo: Official Gamescom preview footage showcases early work-in-progress gameplay running at native 4K resolution with Ultra settings on high-end hardware featuring the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Featured Showcase Level: The official tech demo highlights environmental fidelity and interior lighting within the Egyptian Gallery and Museum level.</p>
