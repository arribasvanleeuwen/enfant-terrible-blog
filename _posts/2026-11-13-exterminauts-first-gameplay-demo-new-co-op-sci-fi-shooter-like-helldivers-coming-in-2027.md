<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Exterminauts is a co-op third person PvE shooter using weaponized power tools and mad-science gadgets to exterminate alien pests. Take on MegaCorp contracts amid energy supremacy clashes, while aliens' thirst for Polymorphic Plasma disrupts plans. Punch In. Clean Up. Get Paid.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">3-Player Blue-Collar Crew: Form a tight-knit trio of gritty, down-to-earth Exterminauts dealing with corporate honey traps and blood-soaked pest control, where every work order costs company money and you must cover your crewmates, tally up your expenses, and survive your shift together.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Weaponized Power Tools: Unleash industrial-grade gunplay using heavy-duty utility and repair equipment repurposed for slaughter, and activate the multi-purpose Overdrive system to turn typical blue-collar gear into devastating, alien-shredding weapons.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">A Hostile Alien Ecosystem: Invade Tian, a living, breathing world that fights back with alien threats that constantly adapt to your squad's tactics, forcing your 3-player team to communicate, reposition, and change strategies.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Cross-Platform Online Co-Op: Team up across platforms in environments combining industrial machinery with Tian's alien ecosystem.</p>
