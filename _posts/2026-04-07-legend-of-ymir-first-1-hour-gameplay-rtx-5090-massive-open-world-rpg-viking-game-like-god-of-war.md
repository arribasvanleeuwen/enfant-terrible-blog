  <div class="description-container"><h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Legend of YMIR is a next-gen Unreal Engine 5 MMORPG that blends the visceral action of God of War with large-scale multiplayer mechanics. It offers players the choice between a traditional experience on Steam or a blockchain-integrated economy via the WEMIX platform.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Legend of YMIR is a next-generation MMORPG where you have to master skill-based combat, lead your clan to glory in massive server wars, and carve your name among the legends. The game is deeply inspired by Norse mythology. It centers on the fall of the gods and the chaos that follows, where players must forge their own destiny in a world teetering on the edge of Ragnarok. The lore revolves around the giant Ymir, whose body formed the foundations of the world. The story focuses on preventing (or surviving) the cycle of destruction.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Cinematic Visceral Combat: The gameplay is designed to feel "weighty" and impactful, intentionally moving away from the "floaty" combat mechanics typically found in older, mobile-first MMOs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Steam Version (Web2): Launched as a "pure" gaming experience, this version removes all blockchain and crypto elements to comply with Steam’s policies. It focuses entirely on traditional MMORPG progression and massive server-wide wars.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The WEMIX PLAY Version (Web3): This version features a Play-to-Earn (P2E) ecosystem, including tokenomics (G-WEMIX) and NFT item minting. It also introduces "Partner Servers" where community leaders and influencers can manage and earn from their own server environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Rune Fighter: New class archetype introduced alongside the Steam launch. It's the game’s first melee magic dealer. It fills a tactical gap between a traditional tank and a glass-cannon mage by using ancient runes to buff allies while dealing devastating close-quarters damage.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Large-Scale Server Wars: The endgame is centered on massive PvP. A "Matching System" reshuffles servers every four weeks to prevent any single clan from dominating indefinitely and to keep the competition fresh.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Crown’s Authority: High-ranking leaders can earn "The King's Gift" or "Declaration of Valhalla," granting them the power to trigger server-wide buffs or distribute rewards to their followers.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming April 7, 2026 to PC as a free-to-play title.</p>
