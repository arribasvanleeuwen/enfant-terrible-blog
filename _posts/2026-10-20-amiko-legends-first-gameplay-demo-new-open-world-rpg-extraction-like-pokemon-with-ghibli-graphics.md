<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Amiko Legends is an upcoming anime-inspired action adventure RPG set in a sprawling world teeming with elemental spirits, ancient ruins, and legendary creatures called Amikos. Players step into the role of a traveler embarking on a grand quest to uncover forgotten realms, harness elemental powers, and fight alongside their Amiko companions to protect the land from an corrupting shadow force.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Partner-Based Combat: Fight alongside your Amiko companions in dynamic real-time battles, combining physical combos, elemental abilities, and synchronized partner attacks.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Creature Bonding & Synergy: Discover, recruit, and raise diverse Amikos with distinct skill sets and element types to tailor your combat strategies for every encounter.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Vibrant Open World Exploration & Extraction: Traverse diverse biomes, from lush forests and floating islands to ancient temple ruins, using companion-assisted movement mechanics like gliding, swimming, and climbing. Extract everything you loot and every creature you catch.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep RPG Progression: Custom-build your character and companion team with upgradeable gear, skill trees, and elemental fusion capabilities to overcome challenging boss encounters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">PVP and Co-op PvE: The demo features a PvP mode and the game will also launch with a PvE Co-op mode.</p>
