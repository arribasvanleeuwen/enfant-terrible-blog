*_Description:_*

You play as Aeta, a battle-hardened soldier traveling through the rugged Apennine Mountains. Unlike a typical knight's homecoming, your mission is personal and desperate: you are searching for your kidnapped friend, Bianca. As you track her captors, you must navigate a world rotting from the plague and consumed by religious fanaticism.

*_Gameplay Features:_*

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 8px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 1.3em;">- The Combat System: It is technical, demanding, and grounded in Historical European Martial Arts (HEMA). Stance Switching: You must fluidly switch between one-handed and two-handed grips. This isn't just for show (certain enemies can only be damaged or staggered if you are in the correct stance). Perfect Attacks: Timing is everything. By syncing your inputs with the moment of impact, you trigger "Perfect Attacks" that break enemy guards and maintain your offensive flow. Directional Parries: You have to actively read enemy movements to block or parry from the correct direction, making every duel a high-stakes game of chess.</h3>

- Ultra Realistic Atmosphere and Visuals: Set during the peak of the Black Death in 14th-century Italy, the game blends brutal historical realism with a folk horror atmosphere. The game uses a bleak, "memento mori" aesthetic. You will explore abandoned mountain villages, claustrophobic forests, and shrines where survivors have left Ex Votos (religious offerings) in a futile attempt to ward off the pestilence. The horror is psychological and environmental rather than supernatural, focusing on the desperation of humanity at its breaking point.
