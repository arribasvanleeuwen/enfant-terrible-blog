  <div class="description-container">*_Description:_*

You play as the lone remaining employee of Sunshine Industries, tasked with accompanying a skyscraper-sized, quadrupedal robot across a lethal desert. This machine is your only lifeline; its massive silhouette provides the only sanctuary from a sun that causes instant radiation death.

*_Gameplay Features:_*

- Third-Person Navigation: The camera stays behind your character, allowing you to precisely judge your distance from the edges of the robot's shadow as it moves across the terrain.

- The Scavenging Loop: During the day, you must time "dashes" into nearby ruins to find parts, food, and water. You have to calculate the robot’s speed to ensure you can return to its shadow before the sun kills you.

- Night Survival: When the sun sets, the robot stops to vent heat. The desert temperature drops to lethal lows, forcing you to huddle near the robot’s cooling vents to stay warm and perform maintenance.

- The Parent-Child Bond: The heart of the game is the evolving relationship between the player and the silent machine. Through repairs and navigation assistance, you develop a bond with the robot, which the developers describe as a "child following in a parent's footsteps" that will remind you of The Last Guardian, Pragmata, The Last of Us and God of War.
