<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Into the Fire is an intense single player extraction survival adventure set in Dante’s Archipelago. It tells the story of an explorer facing an active cataclysm, deploying retro-futuristic inventions to outrun firestorms and navigate crumbling landscapes. Rescue lies at the absolute heart of the experience, delivering a high-pressure loop where every dynamic hazard forces players to quickly adapt to a constantly reshaping environment.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Rescue Narrative: Step into the shoes of a bold explorer tasked with saving as many lives (people and animals alike) as possible from a devastating volcanic disaster.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Volcanic Hazard System: Experience semi-open disaster zones where a dynamic volcano completely carves, changes, and reshapes the level layout and environmental hazards between runs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Retro-Futuristic Inventions: Fight back against spreading, living firestorms and discover buried secrets using an arsenal of unique retro-punk tools and tools of traversal.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hub Management and Extraction Tension: Every decision made in the field creates real tension under strict time pressure, directly affecting who survives and how your operational HUB evolves.</p>
