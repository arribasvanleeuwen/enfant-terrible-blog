<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in New York City in 2012, Blackwood follows Anton Blackwood as he manages a complex double life. By day, he runs a small Brooklyn DVD store, managing inventory, suggesting movies to customers, and taking care of the store cat. By night, he steps into his basement den to take on hitman contracts, customize his arsenal of over 30 weapons, and execute calculated assassinations.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Focused Linear Campaign: Experience a cinematic, story-driven single player adventure, built around carefully designed encounters and structured narrative arcs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Act-Based Story Structure: Act 1 features 4 main story missions alongside multiple side missions and extra content, offering over 6 hours of gameplay in a fully polished, standalone release.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">DVD Store Simulator Mechanics: Balance the action by managing and running a detailed DVD store, handling customer requests, organizing inventory, and immersing yourself in daily shopkeeping activities.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Evolving Tactical Combat: Master varied combat mechanics, execute dynamic takedowns, and utilize a growing arsenal of weapons against diverse enemy types.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanding Content & Roadmaps: Experience ongoing updates between major acts, bringing new side missions, collectibles, achievements, weapon options, and enemy variations.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Act 1 launches September 16, 2026 on PC. Future releases include Act 2 (Planned for March 2027) and Act 3 (Planned for September 2027).</p>
