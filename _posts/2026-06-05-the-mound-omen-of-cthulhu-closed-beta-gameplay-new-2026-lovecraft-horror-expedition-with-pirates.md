<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">The Mound: Omen of Cthulhu is an upcoming co-op first person horror expedition game that mixes the team-based tension of Left 4 Dead and Hunt: Showdown with Lovecraftian cosmic dread. Players must manage their sanity and reality-warping distortions while exploring a shifting cursed jungle and returning to their Galleon ship base for upgrades.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Venture into a handcrafted world inspired by H.P. Lovecraft where a cursed, shifting jungle whispers of cosmic cataclysms. Master the art of survival and forge your path as a group of explorers searching for ancient relics and treasures, managing your sanity and choices that shape how you survive against unholy entities in this first person horror game.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-op Under Pressure: Experience a tactical blend of brutal encounters against unholy creatures and mind-bending phenomena. Survival hinges on teamwork, but "The Madness" intensifies hallucinations; a single misplaced shot or a moment of hesitation can lead to total mission failure.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Psychological Horror Setting: Explore atmospheric, oppressive environments heavily inspired by Lovecraftian cosmic horror. Experience a world that reacts to your fear where reality distorts in real-time, sowing paranoia and doubt through unexplained phenomena and terrifying monsters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Expeditions: Structured design featuring expedition-heavy maps, a treacherous underground world, and a shifting jungle. Push deeper into the unknown for better rewards, but manage your sanity carefully; the further you go, the harder it is to make it back alive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Galleon Hub: Shape your ultimate preparation before every mission by gearing up aboard your hub ship, the Galleon. Work together to escort an ox cart to carry your hard-earned loot and treasures back to safety to fine-tune your equipment for the next run.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming July 15, 2026 for PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
