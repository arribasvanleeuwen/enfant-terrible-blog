Check out our exclusive final review of Monster Hunter Stories 3 Twisted Reflection (developed in RE Engine) after spending many hours exploring its world. This video includes our final impressions along with never-before-seen native 4K gameplay captured on RTX 5090 using Ultra settings during the opening hours of the adventure. Monster Hunter Stories 3 continues Capcom’s turn-based RPG spin-off set in the Monster Hunter universe, expanding the formula introduced in previous entries with a larger world, deeper monster-raising systems and a cinematic story.

*_Our Review: A Must-Play for fantasy RPG lovers in 2026_*

📝 SUMMARY

Monster Hunter Stories 3 is the definitive evolution of the series, masterfully blending Pokemon-style creature collecting with Final Fantasy's cinematic storytelling. Powered by the RE Engine, it delivers a technically polished and deep RPG experience that thrives on both high-end PCs and portable devices.

🖥️ PC ULTRA SETTINGS

To push the RTX 5090 to its limit, we set everything to Ultra/Maximum at native 4K, ensuring Variable LOD is disabled to eliminate pop-in. This configuration guarantees a flawless 240 FPS experience with razor-sharp textures and explosive particle effects for the ultimate visual journey.

🐉 A MASTERPIECE REBORN

Monster Hunter Stories 3 feels like the perfect culmination of a sub-series that has often lived in the shadow of the main franchise entries. While the core franchise focuses on real-time hunting action, the Stories series fully embraces the RPG genre with turn-based combat closer to classic Japanese role-playing games. From the opening cinematic, the game hooks you with its stunningly seamless transitions between exploration and combat, and a deeply personal narrative. The game tackles complex ecological themes and character motivations similar to other JRPGs like Tales of Arise or Final Fantasy.

🎮 A LIVING WORLD THAT PLAYS BEAUTIFULLY ON HANDHELD

Visually, Monster Hunter Stories 3 uses Capcom’s versatile RE Engine, which adapts surprisingly well to a colorful anime-inspired art style. We played the game on PC using an RTX 5090 running at native 4K and 240 FPS.

Performance on Steam Deck is also notable. With some settings adjusted to medium, the game maintains around 40–45 FPS, making it a comfortable experience in portable mode. In many ways, the handheld format feels like a natural fit for this game.

⚔️ POKEMON MEETS FINAL FANTASY

One of the most impressive feats of this entry is its ambitious open world structure, which ditches the fragmented zones of the past for a seamless experience reminiscent of Xenoblade Chronicles 3. Exploration is no longer a chore; with the new Habitat Restoration system, you aren't just passing through environments. You’re actively healing the ecosystem, which keeps the world-building feeling purposeful and dynamic.

The combat remains accessible, but don't let the colorful exterior fool you. Once you dig into the Wyvernsoul Gauge and the Synchro Rush attacks, the tactical depth rivals the hardcore team-building found in Shin Megami Tensei V: Vengeance. It’s a thoughtful RPG that rewards pattern recognition over mindless grinding.

The monster capturing and breeding system carries the addictive spark from Pokemon, but with a much more sophisticated genetic layer. Experimenting with the Rite of Channeling to inherit rare traits feels like true mad-science, allowing you to craft a team that is uniquely yours.

🤝 BONDS THAT MATTER

The Companion Quests are the true standout. While objectives are simple, the writing has taken a huge leap, channeling the "Support Bond" energy of Fire Emblem. These aren't just NPCs; they are vital allies whose evolving stories make the journey feel deeply personal rather than just a checklist.

Narratively, this game improves Capcom's catalogue. The story leans heavily into the cinematic gravity of a modern Final Fantasy, featuring a fully voiced protagonist. The tone is strikingly mature, dealing with themes of war and extinction that provide a gripping contrast to the game’s vibrant, anime-inspired aesthetic.

Monster Hunter Stories 3 is a masterclass in RPG refinement, blending Fire Emblem's character bonds with a technically flawless open world. It’s a definitive evolution that proves the series can stand toe-to-toe with the genre's biggest titans.
