<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Molokaust is a a first person survival horror game set in the ruthless, cursed town of Tecka, you step into the shoes of a desperate father pushed to his absolute limits. A community once normal has been entirely corrupted by an unknown evil. To save your wife and daughter, you must venture deep into the heart of darkness, confront your worst fears, and unravel the town's dark secrets before it claims your family forever.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Oppressive Hyper-Realistic Atmosphere: Immerse yourself in a relentless, terrifying world pulsating with dynamic events, deep secrets, and unpredictable psychological threats lurking in every shadow.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Extreme Resource Management: Keeping your weapons loaded and your inventory meticulously organized is a matter of life and death. <p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tense Exploration & Scavenging: Scavenge every corner of the gritty environment to find scarce resources. Every decision on what to carry or leave behind will test your sanity and survival instincts.</p>
