<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">ReBlade: The Death Spiral is an upcoming action roguelike set in a cyberpunk world deeply inspired by Chinese mythology. You play as a condemned Synthetic marked for destruction and trapped in an endless cycle of resets. In this desolate, tech-fantasy city, you must harness weapons, chips, and augments to cut through mechanical creatures, meet surviving allies, and piece together the hidden truth to break free from your fate.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Master the Rhythm of Combat: Experience a tactical combat system focused on timing, control, and precision. Balance offense and defense, execute parries, read enemy behavior, and strike at the right moment to claim victory.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Shape Your Build, Break Past Limits: Refine your combat strategy with every run. Combine modular weapons (such as the Shield Axe), tactical chips, and cybernetic augments to craft specialized builds adapted to new trial challenges.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Encounter Beasts & Survive the Trials: Face ancient mythological creatures that have been twisted and reinforced by machinery. Study their attack patterns, shatter their defenses, and survive increasingly difficult trial tiers.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Defy Your Fate Through Persistent Progress: Trapped in endless resets, use what you learn in each run to push further. Uncover resources, trade with allies in the world, and permanently enhance your Synthetic character to dismantle the cycle of destruction.</p>
