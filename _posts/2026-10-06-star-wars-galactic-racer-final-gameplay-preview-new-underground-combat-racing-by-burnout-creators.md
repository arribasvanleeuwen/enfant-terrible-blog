<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This Unreal Engine 5 Star Wars racing game, developed by former Burnout creators, combines high-speed arcade action with a narrative-driven sports drama and a Slay the Spire-style roguelite structure.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Star Wars Galactic Racer follows a mysterious masked driver named Shade during the New Republic Era, just after the events of Return of the Jedi. With the war over, former Rebel and Imperial pilots and mechanics channel their leftover military expertise and surplus gear into the high-speed, underground world of hot rod racing. Entering the newly formed Galactic League, created by a dreamer and old pod mechanic named Darius Pax, Shade must navigate a fiercely competitive galaxy to dethrone the egotistical reigning champion, Kestar Bool.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Brutal Combat Racing: Slam, shunt, and trigger devastating takedowns against rival pilots across high-speed unsanctioned tracks.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Runs-Based Solo Campaign: Navigate a branching story map set in the New Republic Era, balancing risk and reward to earn status in the underground Galactic League.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Scrap Customization: Build and upgrade repulsorcraft using salvaged junker parts to create synergistic performance builds.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Four Unique Repulsorcraft Classes: Master distinct vehicle handling models, from agile Speeder Bikes and low-profile Skim Speeders to heavy Landspeeders and fragile, ultra-fast Podracers.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Multi-Path Tracks: Race through iconic Outer Rim planets featuring elevation drops, organic hazards, and multiple viable racing lines.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Arcade & Online Multiplayer: Drop into standalone time trials and scenario modes, or test your skills in 12-player PvP online circuits.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 6, 2026 to PC (Steam), PS5 and XBOX.</p>
