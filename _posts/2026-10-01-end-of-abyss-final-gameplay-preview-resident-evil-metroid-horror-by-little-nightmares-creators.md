<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Step into a dark, subterranean research compound as a young combat technician fighting for survival in this atmospheric sci-fi horror adventure inspired by Resident Evil and Little Nightmares.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">In End of Abyss, players descend into the derelict halls of a sprawling underground complex where a mysterious investigation quickly turns into a harrowing escape. Taking on the role of Cel, a young combat technician, you must navigate broken corridors, uncover hidden secrets, and confront horrifying, bio-mechanical threats that lurk in the dark. Combining survival action with utility-gated Metroidvania exploration, players use Cel's scanner and upgradable arsenal to survive monstrous creatures and push back against the crushing pressure of the facility.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Sci-Fi Survival & Combat: Control combat technician Cel, utilizing precise aiming and resource management to defeat twisted monstrosities.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Metroidvania Exploration: Use your Scanner and utility tools to uncover lost paths, unlock sealed doors, and backtrack through the facility as you grow stronger.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Upgradable Arsenal: Modify and power up your firearms and tech to adapt against increasingly dangerous threats.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Oppressive Atmosphere: Experience a haunting, high-contrast visual world built in Unreal Engine 5.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Crafted by Industry Veterans: Built by Section 9 Interactive, a Sweden-based studio formed by core developers from Little Nightmares.</p>
