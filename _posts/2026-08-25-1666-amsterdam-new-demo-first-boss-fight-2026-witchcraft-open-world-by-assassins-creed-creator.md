<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Check out our exclusive preview of 1666: Amsterdam, the long-awaited dark historical action adventure from Assassin's Creed creator Patrice Désilets. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in Amsterdam during the plague-ridden year of 1666, the city’s golden age masks a terrifying debt owed to ancient entities known as the Originals. You play as Noa Brooklyn, raised for a dark fate as the Collector, alongside Aaron, a man from 1999 linked to her through the eyes of a cat.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Exceptionally Solid Narrative & Characters: You connect with the cast almost immediately. The storytelling is easily the strongest pillar here, weaving magic, mystery, and personal stakes seamlessly into a narrative that pulls you in right from the opening hour.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Classic Structural Vibe: Rather than forcing a massive, bloated open world, the game opts for a tighter, district-based structure reminiscent of classic story-driven games from the 2000s. It keeps the pacing focused and intentional.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Vision of Patrice Désilets: As a master of modern action adventure games, the original Assassin's Creed creator’s signature shines through everywhere. Playing it makes you realize what a powerhouse AAA title this could have been with a blockbuster budget behind it, reminiscent of its original scope back when the project was first pitched at THQ years ago.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deeply Atmospheric World Design: Stepping into 1666 Amsterdam feels incredibly immersive. The contrast between the vibrant day-to-day city life and the claustrophobic, plague-ridden alleys creates an uneasy tension that keeps you on edge from the very start.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unique Day & Night Loop: The gameplay loop feels distinct, daytime focuses on slow-burn detective work, eavesdropping, and tracking demonic targets in plain sight, while nighttime turns into a fast-paced hunt utilizing dark witchcraft.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fascinating Animal Mechanics: Controlling the cat for stealth, vertical exploration, and scouting feels like a dark, witchy blend of Stray and Dishonored. It seamlessly handles vertical exploration, stealth, scouting, and puzzle-solving, giving a fresh perspective on urban navigation.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Intriguing Multi-Era Premise: The interconnected timeline mechanic shows massive potential. Seeing how Noa's actions in the past actively alter the city's layout and clues for Aaron and Clio in the future gives the campaign a unique puzzle-like narrative structure reminiscent of Dishonored 2 or The Medium.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fun Gameplay with Early Access Rough Edges: The game enters Early Access with a solid 15-hour campaign chunk, a generous starting point for players to test the core mechanics while Panache spends roughly a year polishing content toward the full 1.0 release. The core gameplay loop is genuinely fun, but the Early Access state shows mostly on the technical side. You will encounter occasional bugs, particularly around character movement and traversal, though the foundation shows massive promise as the team continues polishing.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Releasing in Early Access on August 25, 2026 on PC (Steam), with full 1.0 release and console versions planned for approximately one year later.</p>
