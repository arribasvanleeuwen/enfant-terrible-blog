<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">EXODUS is a single player third person sci-fi action RPG where players take on the role of a Traveler in a far-future universe. Navigating a hostile galaxy dominated by advanced alien species, players must fight for humanity's survival while grappling with the real-time consequences of interstellar travel.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mass Effect Heritage & Fresh Narrative: This is the direct and undeniable point of comparison. Developed by ex-BioWare veterans (such as Drew Karpyshyn and James Ohlen), the game shares the exact same DNA. Third person cover combat, squad synergy with orderable companion abilities, a mature space opera atmosphere, witty character-driven humor, and deep decision-making.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expansive Arsenal & Gadgets: Playing through an entire new mission called "Liberating the Kay", the game features a vast array of high-tech gadgets, offensive tools, and abilities, offering high combat variability and creative strategy.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Awakened Kay: A community of bio-engineered, highly intelligent, and talkative amphibian allies who traverse space stations in massive water tubes and use translation devices to speak. From enthusiastic young tree-frogs like 42C (who blares music at full blast while guiding you) to grumpy, condescending senior engineers like the toad 13A, these Awakened creatures bring rich personality and humor.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Companion Directives: Combat relies heavily on squad synergy. Issuing real-time orders to your distinct squadmates allows you to complement your playstyle, whether leveraging brute force or stealth tactics.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stellar Visuals & Memorable Writing: The levels feature spectacular, highly detailed designs, paired with exceptionally strong writing, witty dialogue, and memorable characters that echo classic sci-fi charisma.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Time Dilation Mechanics: Travel across interstellar space causes decades to pass back home, fundamentally altering your choices, relationships, and the fate of civilization over generations.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming on April 7, 2027 to PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
