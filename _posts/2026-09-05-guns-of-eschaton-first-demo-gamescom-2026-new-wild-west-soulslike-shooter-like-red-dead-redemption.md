<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Check out our exclusive preview of Guns of Eschaton, an Unreal Engine 5 soulslike FPS that fuses the occult Western vibes of Red Dead Redemption with the fast-paced, spell-slinging combat of Witchfire.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in a haunted Old South transformed by a apocalyptic catastrophe known as "The Burning", Guns of Eschaton puts you in the boots of a fragile gunslinger trying to navigate a land of dust, blood, and occult horror. Armed with authentic 19th-century firearms, custom ammo types, and mystical abilities, you must study your enemies and carefully calculate every shot to survive against monstrous factions and ancient forces.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">First Person Soulslike Combat: Delivers a deliberate, lethal FPS experience where raw reflexes aren't enough. Seamlessly fusing soulslike discipline with shooter mechanics, timed parries, stamina management, and evasive dashes give combat an exceptionally intense rhythm.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visceral Art Direction & World Design: Envisioned under the striking art direction of Viktor Antonov (Half-Life 2, Dishonored), the game's burning 19th-century wasteland blew us away, blending grim occult imagery with haunting, apocalyptic Western architecture.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">19th-Century Arsenal & Gunslinger Mechanics: Wield over 20 brutal firearms crafted with a satisfying, weight-filled feel. Players can treat revolver cylinders as tactical timelines, manually chambering specialized rounds to exploit specific enemy vulnerabilities on the fly.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Occult Builds & Sacramental Powers: Forge highly customized builds using talismans, passive abilities, and mystic spells from three distinct sacramental paths. Weaving dark magic together with heavy gunpowder creates a versatile, spell-slinging combat loop reminiscent of Witchfire.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Cherokee Codex & Tactical Study: Utilize an intricate, hand-drawn codex to analyze enemy anatomy, behavioral patterns, and ritualistic weaknesses. Turning deep occult lore into a practical tactical weapon, this mechanic rewards thorough preparation before entering battle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Solo, Co-Op & PvP Modes: Experience the haunting story campaign alone, team up with friends for full co-op progression, or invade other players' sessions as a hostile threat; a dynamic feature that brings authentic soulslike multiplayer tension to an apocalyptic FPS.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming in 2026 to PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
