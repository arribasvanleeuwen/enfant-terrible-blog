<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">The Sinking City 2 is an upcoming Unreal Engine 5 survival horror game set in a flooded, supernatural 1920s Arkham. It blends classic resource management and tense exploration with an oppressive Lovecraftian atmosphere.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Sinking City 2 is set in a flooded version of 1920s Arkham consumed by supernatural forces, decay, and madness. A supernatural flood has driven out all but the most deranged residents, leaving eldritch horrors to roam the streets. The story follows Calvin Rafferty, an occult adventurer whose cavalier actions have led to devastating consequences. After a dreaming ritual goes wrong, his girlfriend, Faye, is left trapped in a mysterious sleep. Now, Calvin must navigate the drowned streets of a derelict city in a desperate, personal attempt to bring her back, while coming face-to-face with a plague of otherworldly creatures known as the Slither.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Survival Horror Focus: The sequel shifts toward a classic survival horror approach featuring third person combat, limited inventory management, and safe rooms.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Explore Decaying Arkham: Navigate interconnected districts of a flooded, semi-open world city by foot and by boat, exploring rotting mansions, flooded markets, and abandoned hospitals.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Combat and Resource Scarcity: Use an arsenal of 1920s-inspired firearms and melee weapons to fight against Eldritch abominations. Ammunition and resources are strictly limited, encouraging you to balance a finite inventory and use environmental hazards against enemies.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Accessible Detective System: Investigation mechanics return as a lighter, highly rewarding, but largely optional layer. Players can manually link clues on a metaphysical board to uncover secrets, upgrades, locker combinations, or enemy weaknesses.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Modern Map Navigation: The game adopts a convenient map style where rooms change color once you have found all the items inside.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming to August 18, 2026 to PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S.</p>
