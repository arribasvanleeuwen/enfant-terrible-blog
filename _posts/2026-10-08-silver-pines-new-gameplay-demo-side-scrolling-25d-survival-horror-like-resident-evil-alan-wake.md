<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You control Red Walker, a troubled private investigator sent to Silver Pines, a half-abandoned, half-forgotten small American town, to search for a missing musician named Eddie Velvet. As the investigation uncovers the town's dark history, Walker spirals into a surreal, reality-bending nightmare.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Classic Survival Horror Mechanics: Scavenge for scarce supplies, manage a strictly limited inventory, and make every bullet and medical bottle count while fighting or avoiding nightmarish creatures.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Metroidvania-Style Progression: Explore a small, half-abandoned American town full of locked doors and hidden paths. Unlock new tools and weapons to gain access to previously unreachable areas.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Real-Time Tense Interactions: Experience uninterrupted suspense with real-time mechanics (such as manually reloading weapons or typing in door codes) while monsters remain active around you.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Interactive Map & Photo-Tagging System: Navigate complex environments using an in-game map system that allows you to attach photos as visual reminders for locked doors, puzzle locations, and items.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Puzzle-Solving & Investigation: Step into the shoes of private investigator Red Walker, solving environmental puzzles and uncovering clues to untangle the town’s dark secrets.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Varied Enemies & Boss Encounters: Face a wide variety of terrifying monsters and hulking bosses that demand tactical combat or stealth to survive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Distinct Art & Animation Style: Features realistic rotoscoped character animations combined with customized photo-textured environments to deliver high visual depth in a 2.5D side-scrolling format.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">In-World Narrative & Voiced Cutscenes: Deepen your immersion through environmental storytelling and fully voiced, graphic novel-style cutscenes.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming October 8, 2026 to PS5, PC, Xbox and Switch 2.</p>
