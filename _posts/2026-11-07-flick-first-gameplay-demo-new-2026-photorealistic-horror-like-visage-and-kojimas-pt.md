<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Watch this upcoming Unity 6 indie horror game Flick, featuring 4K RTX 5090 gameplay inspired by P.T. and Visage with light-based puzzle mechanics.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Flick is a first person psychological horror game where you flick light switches on and off throughout a long-abandoned house to instantly shift the environment from a safe reality to a blood-soaked echo where grisly puzzles manifest. Every switch of the light is a tense gamble: you need illumination to progress and navigate the oppressive rooms, but turning on the lights can summon the terrifying nightmares and twisted entities hunting you through the dark. Unearth your family's desperate escape and the sickening history of those who resided in the house long before you.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Reality-Shifting Mechanics: Instantly alter your surroundings between a normal environment and a blood-soaked nightmare simply by interacting with light switches.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Calculated Risk & Survival: Balance the absolute necessity of light to progress against the horrors it awakens, as something twisted hunts the echo of your footsteps.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fractured Storyline: Piece together the remnants of a desperate family escape and uncover the dark, disturbing history hidden within the estate.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Immersive, Oppressive Environments: Navigate highly detailed, chilling rooms where atmospheric lighting and deep shadows conceal threats around every corner.</p>
