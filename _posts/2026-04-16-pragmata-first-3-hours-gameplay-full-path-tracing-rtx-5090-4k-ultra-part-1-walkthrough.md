<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This exclusive final preview showcases Capcom's Pragmata running at native 4K Ultra settings on an RTX 5090, highlighting three hours of cinematic sci-fi gameplay and its unique dual-character dynamic. <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">🖥️  ULTRA PC SETTINGS (RTX 5090)</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Resolution: Native 4K</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Anti-Aliasing and Upscaling: Used NVIDIA DLAA for maximum image clarity, combined with DLSS 4 Frame Generation for smoother performance. The game also supports DLSS 4.5 for further performance improvements.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Ray Tracing: Full Path Tracing set to Ultra, supported by Ray Reconstruction to remove noise from the lighting and reflections.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Texture Quality: Set to Max, fully utilizing the high VRAM capacity of the 5090 for crisp surfaces and environment details.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mesh and Geometry: Set to Extreme/Max to ensure zero pop-in during fast-paced lunar movement.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Shadows and AO: Ray Traced Ambient Occlusion and Ultra Shadows provided realistic depth in the moon's craters and station interiors.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Special Effects: Strand-based Hair was enabled for Diana, and Volumetric Lighting was maxed out to enhance the atmospheric space dust effects.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Frame Rate: Stayed consistently above 120+ FPS even in heavy combat sections.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stability: Zero stuttering thanks to RE Engine’s optimization.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Input Lag: Minimal latency thanks to NVIDIA Reflex being active in the background.</p>
