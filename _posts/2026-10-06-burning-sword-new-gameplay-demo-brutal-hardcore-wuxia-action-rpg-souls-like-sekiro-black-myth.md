<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Burning Sword: Death Sun is a wuxia action game set in 12th century China. Play as Zhang Yixian, leader of the Crimson Dust Demon Sect, cast down to the edge of death by Gao Tianqi, master of the Death Sun poison. Carve a bloody path back to him across randomized stages and brutal boss encounters.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hardcore Classic Wuxia Combat: Master brutal melee fights inspired by classic martial arts films, built around high-risk boss duels and relentless offense.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Roguelite Progression & Hub System: Customize skills at shrines, spend earned XP in a growing shop, and tailor your playstyle through unique reward builds on every run.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Burning Sword Mechanism: Build up your Power Gauge with consecutive hits to ignite your blade, dramatically boosting attack power to turn the tide of battle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Qi Powers & Forbidden Qi Drain: Harness Qi for skill casts and ranged blasts, or execute forbidden Qi Drain techniques to steal health and energy directly from foes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Environmental Rebound & Arena Interactions: Launch objects across the combat arena to break enemy guards and open up temporary vulnerabilities.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Qinggong Mobility & Secret Exploration: Wall-run and float through the air using ancient movement techniques, or use special sword skills to access secret regions and hidden paths across biomes.</p>
