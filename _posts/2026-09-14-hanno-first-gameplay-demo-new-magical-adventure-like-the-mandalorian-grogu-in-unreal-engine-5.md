<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Form an unbreakable bond between a young orphan and a magical elephant as you set out to save your world. When dark Asura forces invade the land of Ashar, you must combine human strategy with mythical magic to solve ancient puzzles, defeat corrupt entities, and restore peace to your home. Explore, fight as one, and uncover the truth behind your powers.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expressive Duo Mechanics: Command Vimal and harness Hanno's unique magical skills to manipulate the environment, trigger mechanisms, and solve intricate puzzles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Synergistic Combat System: Fight as one using dynamic magic transfers and combined abilities to defeat corrupt Asura enemies and boss encounters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Skill Trees & Customization: Upgrade combat abilities for both Vimal and Hanno across distinct progression paths, unlocking cosmetics for your elephant companion as you explore.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Rich Mythical World: Explore the vibrant lands of Ashar, featuring breathtaking South Asian-inspired architecture, lush forests, and ancient temple ruins.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Story-Driven Journey: Uncover an emotional narrative centered around grief, friendship, and responsibility as two unlikely heroes fight to protect everything they hold dear.</p>
