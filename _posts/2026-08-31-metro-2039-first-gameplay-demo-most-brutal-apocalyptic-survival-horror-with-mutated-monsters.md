<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Metro 2039 plunges players into the dark heart of a post-apocalyptic Moscow sub-surface. Governed by Hunter's authoritarian regime, the tunnels are locked in a ruthless war. Players navigate the ruined Metro network as the Stranger, a reclusive outsider tormented by nightmares who must venture back into the deep underground to confront the ghosts of his past and survive terrifying mutant threats.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Return to Claustrophobic Survival Horror: Shifts the focus back to tight, dark tunnel systems and oppressive indoor settings. We believe this is the right direction, as the claustrophobic atmosphere and well-placed jump scares feel like a true return to form for the franchise.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Resource & Survival Mechanics: Survival features return with full force. Gas mask filter timers, manual charger pumps for electrical tools, lighter mechanics to clear spiderwebs, and scarce ammunition. All these systems force players into constant tension during combat encounters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visceral Combat & Classic Arsenal: Weapon customization remains grounded and gritty, featuring iconic fan favorites like the makeshift "Bastard Gun" alongside heavy pneumatic weaponry. One of the best features is the heavy recoil and impactful hit feedback, making every bullet fired feel deliberate.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Advanced Next-Gen Visuals: Built on 4A Games' upgraded 4A Engine, the game uses dynamic lighting, ray-traced shadows, and realistic fog effects to create one of the most atmospheric representations of post-apocalyptic Moscow to date</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming in February 2027 to PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
