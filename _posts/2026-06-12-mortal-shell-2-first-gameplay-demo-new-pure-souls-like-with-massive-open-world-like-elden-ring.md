  <div class="description-container"><h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Mortal Shell II is an upcoming dark fantasy souls-like action RPG that expands its predecessor's formula into an interconnected open world. Players step into the role of the Harbinger, using unique body-possession mechanics to inhabit the flesh of fallen warriors and fight against relentless zealots and cosmic aberrations.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The world of Mortal Shell II is a ravaged, twisted landscape ruled by fanatical cults and looming, monstrous entities. Players awaken as the Harbinger, a prophesied being sent on a desperate pilgrimage to reclaim the blessed Ova of the Undermether from the zealots and abominations guarding her unborn kin. To survive this brutal journey, the Harbinger must locate and possess Shells, the dormant physical remains of forgotten warriors scattered across the world. Inhabiting these bodies grants the player their unique skills, combat styles, and lost memories, allowing them to carve a trail through a non-linear world and face formidable foes like Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods, to break the grip of false gods.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Expanded Open World Exploration: Unlike the tighter layout of the original game, the sequel features an immersive, interconnected open world environment. It is designed to be expansive yet deliberately compact, packed with optional dungeons, hidden mini-bosses, and secret lore structures.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">No Stamina Combat: One of the most radical changes to the combat system is the complete removal of the Stamina system. Players are no longer limited by a fatigue gauge when attacking, dodging, or sprinting, resulting in faster-paced, highly aggressive, and fluid tactical encounters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hardening Mechanic: The franchise's defining tactical feature returns. Players can turn their shell into solid stone at any moment (even mid-swing) to absorb crushing enemy blows, stagger attackers, and immediately chain together devastating counter-attacks.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Diverse Playable Shells: Discover an array of unique Shells to match your specific playstyle. Confirmed forms include Tiel (the agile, rogue-like Acolyte), Eredrim (the heavily armored, knightly Venerable), and Proxima (the sinister, horned Broodseeker), each possessing specialized skill trees and distinct weapon masteries.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deeper Weapon and Upgrade Customization: Master a new, versatile arsenal of transforming weapons. The sequel introduces extensive upgrade trees and customizable ability options, allowing players to significantly refine and adapt their combat capabilities as they progress.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming in 2026 to PS5, XBOX and PC.</p>
