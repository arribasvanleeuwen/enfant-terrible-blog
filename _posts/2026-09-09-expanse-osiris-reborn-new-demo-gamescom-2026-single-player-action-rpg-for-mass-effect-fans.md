<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Expanse: Osiris Reborn is a single player third person sci-fi action RPG where players create and customize their own captain (Earther, Martian, or Belter) working for Pinkwater Security. Navigating a fractured solar system on the brink of collapse, players must gather a crew, manage relationships, and survive a solar system-wide conspiracy after a routine stay on Eros Station erupts into a dangerous quarantine crisis.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mass Effect-Style Pacing: Watching the extended demo, Owlcat’s shift from isometric CRPGs to a third person action RPG immediately feels fluid. The mission structures feel tight, snappy, and focused, channeling classic squad shooter dynamics while remaining firmly grounded in the hard sci-fi tone of The Expanse.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Punchy Gunplay & Environmental Strategy: Combat centers around snappy cover-shooter mechanics. Weapons feel heavy and responsive, and the demo highlighted how players can shoot out power junctions, blow up fuel canisters, and use cover destruction to turn chaotic firefights to their advantage.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Dialogue & Choice Consequence: The preview showcased a robust dialogue system with skill checks. How you speak to corporate handlers, station vendors, or Belter scavengers alters your reputation and unlocks distinct resolution paths instead of forcing combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tactical Companion Command: During combat encounters, you direct your squadmates in real time or pause to order specific ability strikes, flush enemies from fortified positions, or send companions to bypass security doors mid-firefight.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Next-Gen Visuals & Atmospheric Cutscenes: Built in Unreal Engine 5, the lighting in zero-g corridors, detailed character gear, and atmospheric particle effects look impressive. Cutscenes build genuine tension, with high-stakes decision points slowing down the presentation for dramatic emphasis.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Frigate Navigation & Station Hubs: Beyond ground skirmishes, the demo walked through hub exploration and ship operations. Navigating your frigate across key sectors like Ceres, Mars, and Ganymede requires managing ship systems, upgrading weapons, and handling interpersonal crew disputes.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming in Q2 2027 to PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.</p>
