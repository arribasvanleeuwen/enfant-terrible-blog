<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This 4K preview showcases an hour of RTX 5090 gameplay from Project Mist, an upcoming survival horror title inspired by STALKER and The Forest and built in Unity 6 that pits players against colossal, Kaiju-like monsters on a hostile island.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Explore a remote island solo or in co-op in this open world survival horror. Manipulate objects and enemies with the Gravity Gun, build a moving train base, fight giant creatures, catch critters, upgrade your gear, and survive a hostile world shaped by abandoned, mysterious facilities.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Moving Base: One of the most unique mechanics is the ability to build and upgrade a moving train base. This allows you to travel across the dangerous island while keeping your resources and crafting stations safe (or as safe as they can be).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Gravity Gun and Weaponry: You have access to a Gravity Gun, which is essential for environmental puzzles and combat. You can also craft traps, siege weapons, and traditional firearms to fend off the island’s nightmares.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Atmospheric Survival: The world is shrouded in a dense, ever-changing fog. You’ll need to manage your hunger, thirst, and stamina while scavenging for resources like "blood orbs" and crafting materials.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">No Forced Quests: The game emphasizes freedom. There are no fixed quest markers telling you where to go; you must explore the secrets behind a giant, mysterious wall at your own pace.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Co-op Gameplay: While it can be played solo, it supports co-op, allowing you to build and survive with friends.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Early Access coming May 19, 2026 to PC.</p>
