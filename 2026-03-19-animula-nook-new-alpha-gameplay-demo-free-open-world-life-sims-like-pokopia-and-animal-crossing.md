<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Animula Nook is a vibrant, free-to-play multiplayer sandbox RPG where you play as a tiny character building a miniature civilization within a massive human world. Running on Unity 6, the game offers a low-stress "healing" experience that blends the cozy life-sim vibes of Pokopia and Animal Crossing with the unique micro-perspective of Grounded and Pikmin.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Shrink down and reimagine your desk as a whimsical world in Animula Nook, a cozy miniature life sim. Craft your homes from odds and ends, grow giant crops, style your OOTD, and explore the enchanting Wupa Woods. Just remember, Minies are always by your side.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Miniature Perspective: Your entire world is set within human spaces, like a bedroom desk, a windowsill, or a houseplant "forest." You navigate these areas by climbing pencils, sliding down books, or riding a tiny skateboard.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Creative Crafting: You use a "shrink gun" to turn human-sized "clutter" into building materials. Milk cartons become houses, teacups transform into bustling cafés, glasses cases are repurposed into sofas, bottle caps serve as rooftops...</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Resource Gathering: Instead of standard ores, you collect "natural" treasures like sunlight (which you suck up with a tiny vacuum), raindrops, and gentle breezes to use as crafting components.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Social Simulation: You share this world with "Minies," unique NPCs you can befriend, dress up in custom outfits, and even form a band with using one of the eight playable musical instruments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming later in 2026 to PS5, PC and Nintendo Switch 2.</p>
