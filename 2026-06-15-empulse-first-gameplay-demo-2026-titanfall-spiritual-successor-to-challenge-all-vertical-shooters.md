<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Empulse is the upcoming spiritual successor to Titanfall from Splitgate developers 1047 Games, running on Unreal Engine 5. The game takes the fast-paced movement mechanics of their previous portal-shooter and pivots to a traditional 6v6 arena shooter format focused on wall-running, grappling hooks, and pilotable mechs.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">High-Speed Traversal: A core focus on advanced mobility where players can chain inputs to reach a seamless "flowstate." Traversal mechanics include forward and backward wall-running on virtually any vertical surface (even below maps), Holojumps, and a versatile Grapple Hook used to whip around corners, swing from pillars, reel in enemies for a one-hit melee, or hitch a ride on teammates and mechs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Alter the Map with Bombs: Replacing traditional grenades, Plasma Activated Infusion of Nano-Tech (P.A.I.N.T.) bombs permanently change surface behaviors. These include Jump bombs for vertical boosts, Speed bombs for momentum, Explosive bombs for delayed tactical bursts, Heal bombs for team recovery, and Sticky bombs to slow down enemies and mechs.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mechs as the Ultimate Power Spike: Two powerful, pilotable mechs spawn at the center of the map as highly contested mid-round objectives that completely shift the tide of 6v6 combat. Their dominant kit includes a ramping chaingun, devastating rockets, a heavy knockback melee, a deployable bubble shield, a rapid dash, and a high-altitude charge jump that slams down with a crushing shockwave. However, foot pilots can counter them by grappling directly onto a mech to land a melee hit that temporarily disables its shield.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Lethal Progression: A gameplay loop focused on mastering advanced pilot abilities and futuristic mechanical progression.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Player-First Launch Model: The game will launch in Early Access at a discounted price with absolutely no monetization, battle passes, or in-game stores. Everything will be unlockable just by playing and completing challenges.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming June 24, 2026 to PS5, XBOX and PC.</p>
