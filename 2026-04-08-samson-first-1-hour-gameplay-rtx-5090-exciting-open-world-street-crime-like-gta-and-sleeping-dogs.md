<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Experience the first hour of Samson, a brutal open world crime game from the creator of Just Cause captured in 4K on an RTX 5090. This Unreal Engine 5 preview showcases gritty melee combat and violent driving in a sandbox inspired by Mad Max and GTA.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You play as Samson McCray, a man forced to return to his unforgiving hometown to settle a massive debt with dangerous criminals. The mob is using Samson’s sister as leverage to ensure he pays up. Unlike most open world games where you can ignore the main quest indefinitely, time is your enemy in Samson. Your debt grows every hour with interest, forcing you to constantly take on risky jobs to meet your Daily Quota.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Debt: It compounds daily. Every hour you’re not working, you’re falling further behind. Tyndalston doesn’t offer payment plans.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Action Points: A limited daily pool keeps the pressure real. Every job is a trade-off. Spend them wrong and tomorrow gets worse.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">No Guns: Fists, elbows, improvised blunt objects, and whatever’s bolted to the wall. Combat is grounded, punishing, and drawn from the same DNA as Sifu, Sleeping Dogs, and The Warriors.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Brutal Driving: The Magnum Opus is Samson’s personal weapon on wheels. Part-based damage, real physics, and the kind of vehicular chaos that made Sundberg famous.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Open City Map: Tyndalston tracks everything. Factions shift. Streets change. The people you cross don’t forget. Your choices are permanent and the city will remind you of them.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Single Player Experience: A focused 10-hour journey (25 for completion).</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming April 8, 2026 to PC. Console versions planned for later.</p>
