<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This work-in-progress Unreal Engine 5 gameplay from the upcoming action sci-fi shooter, Turok Origins, combines elements of Predator and Doom Eternal to deliver an intense dinosaur-hunting and weapon-progression experience.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Turok Origins is an upcoming sci-fi action shooter that can be played solo or co-op with friends. Players step into the shoes of the legendary Turok warriors to explore deadly alien worlds and defend the galaxy against ferocious dinosaurs and a terrifying extraterrestrial threat aiming to wipe out all human life.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual Camera Perspectives: Switch freely between a classic First Person view (featuring quick directional dashes/slides) and a Third Person view (featuring tactical dodge rolls).</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Solo Survival (Single Player): Tackle the entire campaign completely alone with dynamically scaling difficulty and no intrusive AI companion bots holding you back.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">3-Player Campaign Co-op: Team up with up to two friends to conquer the main story missions as a coordinated squad.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Three Primordial Archetypes: Master three unique, spirit-animal-inspired classes to create powerful battlefield synergies: Bison (Tank): Deploys massive energy shields and defensive barriers; Cougar (Skirmisher): Focuses on high mobility, speed, and aggressive close-quarters combat; Raven (Scout): Specializes in long-range tracking and precision combat utilizing the iconic tech-bow.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Ferocious Enemy Variety: Battle a brutal array of dinosaurs, hostile alien creatures, and massive bosses across the ground, sky, hazardous swamps, and dark caves.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Futuristic Sandbox Arsenal: Unlock and upgrade advanced sci-fi weaponry, including plasma rifles, ray guns, sniper rifles, high-tech bows, and heavy shotguns.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Real-Time DNA Suit Evolution: Extract genetic material from fallen foes to visually transform your biomorphic armor and unlock upgradeable active skills.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Epic Sci-Fi Campaign: Journey through the breathtaking Lost Lands across multiple planets, exploring ancient alien temples, wasteland canyons, and dense prehistoric jungles.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming Fall 2026 to PS5, XBOX, Switch 2 and PC.</p>
