<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Created by a solo developer in Unreal Engine 5, Mutation is an upcoming zombie survival horror game that blends the classic map layout and resource management of Resident Evil 1 with the terrifying, immersive first person perspective of Resident Evil 7.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Wake up to find that the entire manor you sneaked into last night is now filled with nothing but the walking dead. As the sole survivor, you must escape this isolated manor of death and uncover the colossal secret hidden behind it all.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Survival Horror: A classic approach to the genre where managing limited resources (ammo, healing items) is crucial to staying alive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">First Person Perspective: The game is played entirely in first person with a fixed FOV (Field of View) to increase the feeling of claustrophobia and tension.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Atmospheric Puzzles: Features straightforward, progression-driven puzzles that players must solve to unlock new areas of the manor.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Indie Project: Developed by a passionate, single indie developer, focusing heavily on tension, atmosphere, and classic survival mechanics.</p>
