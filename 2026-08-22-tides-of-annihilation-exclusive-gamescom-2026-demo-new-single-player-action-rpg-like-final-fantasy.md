<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Tides of Annihilation is an action adventure title based on Arthurian mythology set in a ruined London, which has been torn apart by a cataclysmic Outworld invasion. Players control Gwendolyn, who is fueled by fierce personal vengeance and a desperate mission to save her sister. Her quest takes her across a devastated, fog-shrouded modern metropolis and the mythical realm of Avalon as she unravels the mystery behind her newfound powers and determines the fate of reality itself.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fast-Paced Neo-Medieval Combat: The game features a very stylish character-action system recalling titles like Bayonetta and Final Fantasy XVI. Action relies on rapid aggression, fluid light and heavy attack combos, tight dodge windows, and parries designed to reward momentum and high visual spectacle.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Spectral Knight System: Gwendolyn can equip and instantly swap between four distinct spectral knights in mid-combat. Beyond augmenting weapon attacks with skills like Lamorak's spear thrusts, these knights provide vital traversal tools, such as Bors' zipline arrows or Palamedes' shield bash, seamlessly blending combat combos with environmental traversal.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual-World Exploration & Environmental Puzzles: Exploration seamlessly shifts between distorted real-world landmarks (like the ruined Great Russell Museum) and folded, atmospheric mirror realms. The game features non-linear level structure filled with platforming sections, hidden pathing, environmental puzzles, and optional mini-boss encounters.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Fluid Progression & Weapon Variety: Beyond her base sword, players unlock specialized weaponry and customizable skill trees for both Gwendolyn and her summoned knights. Preview impressions note that mixing elemental attributes with knight abilities allows for extensive build variety tailored to different playstyles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Boss Battles with Balanced Challenge: Featuring multi-phase cinematic encounters against foes like Mordred and Colossal Knights, the gameplay emphasizes spectacle without strict Soulslike friction. Adjustable difficulty options and a dedicated Story Mode ensure the intense boss fights remain engaging for all player skill levels.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming soon to PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.</p>
