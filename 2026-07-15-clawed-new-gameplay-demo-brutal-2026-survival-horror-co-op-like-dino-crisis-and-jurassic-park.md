<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Clawed is an upcoming Unreal Engine 5 co-op survival horror shooter that blends terrifying dinosaur encounters with deep survival mechanics. Heavily inspired by classics like Dino Crisis and Jurassic Park!</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Embark on a covert mission to the dinosaur theme park Prehistoria, where you must explore the park's grounds to scavenge resources, solve puzzles, and fight your way through diverse biomes, underground tunnels and deadly enclosures. But beware, you are not the apex predator here.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Terrifying Dino Encounters: Face off against highly aggressive prehistoric predators, ranging from the swift and cunning Utah Raptor to the massive, swamp-dwelling Spinosaurus.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Intricate Survival-Horror Puzzles: Put your wits to the test with classic-style environmental and logical puzzles that you must solve to unlock new areas of the ruined theme park.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Co-Op Integration: You don't have to face the prehistoric nightmare alone; team up with your friends in online co-op to scavenge, coordinate, and fight together.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Atmospheric Exploration: Uncover the dark secrets of Prehistoria as you navigate overgrown surface biomes, claustrophobic maintenance tunnels, and derelict park attractions.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>
