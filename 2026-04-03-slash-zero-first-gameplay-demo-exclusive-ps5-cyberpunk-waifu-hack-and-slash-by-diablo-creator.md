  <div class="description-container"><h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Powered by Unreal Engine 5, SlashZero is a high-speed 3D anime action-roguelite that fuses a neon-soaked cyberpunk aesthetic with the technical, combo-driven combat of Devil May Cry. Developed by Streetlamp Studio and published by Skystone Games, this upcoming indie game marks the latest venture from the creator of the original Diablo.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">SlashZero is a 3D anime-style roguelike action platformer set in a vibrant, futuristic cyberpunk world where the narrative revolves around a group known as Timehackers. A mysterious entity called HIEROS (the Lord of the Void) has descended, shattering timelines and threatening the world. As a Timehacker, you must leap across divergent timelines to retrieve uncorrupted "Authority Keys," unlock immense power, and uncover the truth behind the Null Void to stop HIEROS.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dynamic Combat: Players can chain together ground and aerial combos, using dashes and vaults to maintain momentum. The combat system emphasizes hit feedback, making every strike feel impactful.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Roguelike Progression: Each run allows players to build unique power-ups using Authority Keys, Blessings, and Codes. These modifiers change how your character fights and moves, ensuring no two runs are identical.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Parkour Movement: Levels are designed with multiple layers, encouraging players to use parkour-style traversal to navigate the environment and gain tactical advantages during battles.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming in 2026 to PC and to PS5 as a console exclusive.</p>
