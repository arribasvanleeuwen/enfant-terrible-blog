  <div class="description-container"><h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">📝 SUMMARY</h3>

<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">The Road of Dust and Sorrow is an upcoming sidescrolling zombie game that combines the mechanical tension of survival horror classics with the emotional depth of modern narrative titles like Resident Evil and The Last of Us.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The game is set a decade after a catastrophic failed experiment. In an attempt to end world famine, humanity created a biological remedy that instead became its ruin, twisting people into feral, ravenous creatures known as Dusters. You play as Katherine, a mother navigating the ruins of civilization. Your primary goal is to protect your daughter, Ava, and keep her alive at all costs while searching for a safer future.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Linear Survival Horror: A focus story-driven gameplay rather than open world wandering.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Protect What Matters: Much of the tension comes from safeguarding Ava. The game forces players to make difficult moral choices, blurring the line between necessity and cruelty.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Exploration and Puzzles: Navigate decaying streets, locked rooms, and forgotten corridors. You’ll need to solve environmental puzzles and manage scarce resources to survive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Handcrafted Pixel Art: The game features a unique, detailed pixel-art style that finds nostalgic beauty in the post-apocalyptic decay.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming soon to PC.</p>
