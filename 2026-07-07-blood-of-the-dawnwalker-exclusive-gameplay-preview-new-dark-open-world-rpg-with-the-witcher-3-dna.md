<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Get an exclusive look at The Blood of Dawnwalker, an upcoming Unreal Engine 5 dark fantasy RPG created by The Witcher 3 original creators. This preview showcases the game's prologue, the first open world quests, and unique nighttime vampire mechanics.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">You play as Coen, a Dawnwalker who exists between humanity and vampirism in a plague-stricken, 14th-century kingdom called Vale Sangora. You have exactly 30 days and nights to save your family from the vampire lord Brencis.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES AND FIRST IMPRESSIONS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dark Fantasy Sandbox: Nestled deep within the rugged Carpathian Mountains, the plague-stricken kingdom of Vale Sangora is a stunningly grim, hand-crafted open world built in Unreal Engine 5. We'd like to highlight the incredible environmental variety, from treacherous swamps and subterranean caves to crumbling medieval ruins and dense primeval forests. More than just a pretty backdrop, the world behaves like a true sandbox; a massive web of side quests and hidden areas offers deep player agency, meaning your journey can drastically deviate from another player's depending on exactly who you choose to speak to.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The 30-Day Ticking Clock: Time is your most precious and scarce resource as you try to save your family from the vampire lord Brencis. Each day-and-night cycle is strictly divided into eight slices. While roaming and exploring won't drain your time, committing to major tasks, pursuing multi-part side quests, or learning new skills will aggressively push the clock forward. You simply cannot save everyone, forcing agonizing strategic choices on who to help.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual-Identity Combat (The Day vs. Night Shift): The core loop of the game completely transforms based on the position of the sun, offering two entirely distinct gameplay genres.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">By Day (Tactical Human): Combat is a methodical tactical experience focused on stamina management and directional inputs (up, down, left, right) to parry and attack, heavily reminiscent of For Honor or Kingdom Come. While an on-screen icon signals the perfect timing for executions, players struggling with the steep learning curve can opt for an automated block-attack at the cost of massive stamina penalties. You'll also utilize Runic Hexes to weaken tougher foes.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">By Night (Character Action Vampire): The game completely shifts gears into a blistering, high-speed character action style. Coen unleashes aggressive claw strikes, supernatural speed, and a vital biting mechanic to siphon health mid-fight. As you surrender to corruption, you unlock devastating dark powers. Nighttime completely revolutionizes traversal as well, allowing you to sprint vertically up walls and teleport to effortlessly reach high-altitude secrets or ambush prey.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">A Highly Reactive World and the Infamy System: The world remembers who you are. NPCs and the environment itself react dynamically based on your current physical form and your previous moral choices. The brilliant Infamy system is tied directly to Brencis's main soldiers. As you actively sabotage and disrupt their local operations throughout Vale Sangora, their anger rise, which organically triggers unique, highly dangerous stalker encounters and exclusive side quests.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Master of the Beasts: Early in the campaign, you unlock the ability to shapeshift into a massive black wolf at night to rapidly traverse the terrain. The developers are brilliantly leaning into classic Dracula folklore, making Coen feel like a true master of the wild rather than just a standard vampire.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming September 3, 2026 to PS5, Xbox and PC.</p>
