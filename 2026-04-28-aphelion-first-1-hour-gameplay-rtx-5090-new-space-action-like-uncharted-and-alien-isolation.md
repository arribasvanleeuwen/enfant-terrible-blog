<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Experience single player space action in this 4K RTX 5090 gameplay preview of Aphelion. Developed in Unreal Engine 5, the game features a dual-protagonist narrative that evokes the action of Uncharted and the atmospheric tension of Alien: Isolation.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in the year 2060, Earth has become uninhabitable. Humanity’s last hope lies with Persephone, a newly discovered frozen ninth planet at the edge of the solar system. You follow two astronauts, Ariane Montclair and Thomas Cross, whose mission goes sideways when their ship, the Hope-01, crash-lands on the surface. Separated and stranded, Ariane must navigate the brutal, shifting landscape to find a wounded Thomas and complete their mission.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Two protagonists with different playstyles.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Ariane: Her segments focus on traversal action and parkour. Using tools like a grappling hook and oxygen tank, she climbs melting ice walls and leaps across dark chasms.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Thomas: Because he is injured, his gameplay is more grounded, focusing on investigation, ingenuity, and puzzle-solving to survive his immediate surroundings.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stealth and Horror: The game incorporates tense stealth elements. The duo is hunted by a mysterious, predatory entity known as the Nemesis, creating a "cat-and-mouse" atmosphere inspired by the Alien franchise.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming April 28, 2026 to PS5, PC and Xbox Game Pass Day 1.</p>
