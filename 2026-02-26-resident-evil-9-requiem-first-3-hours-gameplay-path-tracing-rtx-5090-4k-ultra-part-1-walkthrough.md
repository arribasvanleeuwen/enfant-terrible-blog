<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 PC MAX SETTINGS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">4K Ultra | Path Tracing ON | DLSS 4.5 Frame Gen</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Ray Tracing Quality: Set to Full Path Tracing</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Impact: This is the first RE Engine title to feature unified path-traced lighting, providing photorealistic reflections, global illumination, and terrifyingly accurate shadows in dark corridors.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Performance and Upscaling (2026 Tech): NVIDIA DLSS 4.5, Frame Generation and Ray Reconstruction to maintain fluid motion despite the heavy Path Tracing load.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">DLAA (Deep Learning Anti-Aliasing): Used for maximum image sharpness at native resolution.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Reflex Low Latency: Enabled + Boost to ensure responsive combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Advance Texture Quality: Ultra (8GB+) – Pushing high-res assets for every zombie wound and environment detail.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mesh Quality: Max – Increasing geometric complexity for smoother character models.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hair Strands: On – High-fidelity physics for realistic hair movement.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Variable Rate Shading (VRS): Balanced/Off (to prioritize pure visual quality over performance).</p>
