<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Occultist is a first-person narrative thriller. You play as Alan Rebels (voiced by Doug Cockle, the iconic voice behind Geralt of Rivia in The Witcher series), a paranormal investigator who travels to an abandoned British island after his father's disappearance. Explore Godstone island, face supernatural entities, and uncover long-buried occult rituals.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Mystic Pendulum: The most important object for Alan. Through it, he is able to interact with the environment and modify it due to its four unique and original mechanics.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">First Person Action: Experience the foreboding world of The Occultist through the eyes of Alan Rebels and use the mystic pendulum to unravel what happened in GodStone.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Survival Horror: The Occultist has all the distinctive elements of the genre, with tense exploration and atmosphere, as well as an up-close and personal narrative.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Next-Gen Graphics: The Occultist has an astonishing level and quality of graphics running in Unreal Engine 5 for a truly immersive horror experience.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hide and Sneak: Alan always avoids direct combat. Using stealth mechanics, he must advance through the story without being caught off-guard.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Memorable characters: Godstone is populated with adversaries that will make your investigation a real nightmare.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Puzzles and Paranormal Detective Work: From straightforward puzzles to those that require knowledge and insight into the occult arts.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Single Player Experience: Average playtime 8-10 hours.</p>
