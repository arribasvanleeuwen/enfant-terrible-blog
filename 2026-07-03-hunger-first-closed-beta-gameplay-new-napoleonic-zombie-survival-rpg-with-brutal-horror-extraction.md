<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hunger is an upcoming multiplayer first person extraction RPG set in the dark, chaotic aftermath of a plague-ravaged Napoleonic Europe. A bacterium of unknown origin has twisted the continent into a desolate wasteland filled with ruin, fear, and unspeakable horrors known as "the Hunger." Players step into the shoes of unique survivors venturing out on desperate, high-risk expeditions to scavenge resources, build their reputation, and outlast both terrifying monsters and rival survivors.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Hybrid Shooting and Slashing Combat: The game features a methodical yet frenetic combat system that combines firearms and melee weapons. Players can deliver fire using early rifles, pistols, or experimental hand mortars, and fluidly transition to axes or maces for lethal hand-to-hand combat when ammunition runs dry or spaces close in.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Character and Mastery RPG Progression: Players can level their characters by completing quests and surviving expeditions. Each survivor features unlockable Passive, Tactical, and Ultimate abilities, alongside deep and highly customizable mastery trees containing more than 100 talents to shape specific playstyles like solo stealth or team support.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Chateau Hub and Economy: Between expeditions, players gather at a multiplayer hub world called the Chateau. Here, players interact with other survivors, take on detailed quests from NPCs to build reputation, craft gear, use a player-driven marketplace/auction house, and manage their Stash and Vault to secure precious items and weapons away from the dangers of the field.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Engaging PvPvE and End-Game Content: The core extraction loop expands significantly into the endgame. Surviving players can test their builds and teamwork in varied endgame challenges, including dangerous multi-environment dungeons and intense boss encounters.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Coming soon to PC via Steam and the Epic Games Store, where it will launch initially in Early Access.</p>
