<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Set in New York City in 2012, Blackwood follows Anton Blackwood as he manages a complex double life. By day, he runs a small Brooklyn DVD store, managing inventory, suggesting movies to customers, and taking care of the store cat. By night, he steps into his basement den to take on hitman contracts, customize his arsenal of over 30 weapons, and execute calculated assassinations.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Dual-Life Roleplay: Balance quiet routines by running a Brooklyn DVD store by day, then step into the underworld as a contract killer by night.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unmatched Takedown System: Perform countess contextual finishers including environmental, melee, and cinematic executions.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Grounded & Stylish Combat: Utilize cover mechanics, diving, sliding, and over 30 unique customizable weapons in tactical shootouts.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Detailed 2012 New York: Explore an authentic, gritty depiction of NYC across specific narrative-driven missions selected from your basement terminal.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Deep Hub Customization: Manage store operations, customize firearms at your workbench, interact with NPCs, and equip your store cat with unlockable collars.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Focused Story-Driven Design: Replayable linear missions with custom modifiers instead of a pure open world map.</p>
