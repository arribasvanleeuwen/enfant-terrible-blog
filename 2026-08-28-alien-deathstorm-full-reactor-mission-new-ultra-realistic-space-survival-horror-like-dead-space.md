<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Built for fans of immersive action horror and relentless sci-fi tension, Alien Deathstorm follows an Engineer on a dangerous rescue mission turned survival struggle across an alien colony overrun by horrific extraterrestrial forces.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">First Person Action Horror: Delivers a tense FPS survival experience filled with horror elements and intense combat.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">The Reactor Mission: Features a high-pressure objective where players must navigate a malfunctioning fission reactor under extreme conditions to retrieve a key Initiator.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Relentless Extraterrestrial Onslaught: Demands tactical combat, careful resource management, and quick reflexes against aggressive alien creatures.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Rescue & Investigation Campaign: Blends exploratory survival mechanics with a story-driven mystery as players uncover what happened to the colony.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✅ RELEASE DATE AND PLATFORMS</h3>
