<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">This Unreal Engine 5 preview showcases Nakwon: Last Paradise, a brutal extraction survival game that combines the stealthy scavenging of Project Zomboid with social management systems. It aims to deliver a big urban open world experience that blends Resident Evil action with the gritty atmosphere of The Last of Us.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Nakwon is a multiplayer zombie survival extraction game set in a devastated city overrun by the infected. Players begin as refugees living in a survivor shelter and must venture into the dangerous urban districts to search for food, materials and equipment needed to stay alive. Every expedition is a high-risk mission where zombies and other survivors compete for the same limited resources. Players must maintain their shelter, craft equipment and prepare for dangerous scavenging runs.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Urban Open World: Explore a city in South Korea overrun by the infected while searching for vital resources.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">PvPvE Expeditions: Face both zombies and other survivors during high-risk missions across dangerous urban zones.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Day and Night Survival Cycle: Manage your shelter during the day and venture into the city at night to scavenge for supplies.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Strategic Survival Choices: Players can hide, escape, cooperate or betray others depending on the situation.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Shelter Management: Build and customize your refuge with furniture, craft items and upgrade your equipment to survive longer.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Realistic Survival Systems: Everyday objects can become weapons, small wounds can be fatal and psychological stress can affect your survival.</p>
