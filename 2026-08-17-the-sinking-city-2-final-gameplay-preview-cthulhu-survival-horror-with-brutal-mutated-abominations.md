<div style="background: #1a1a1a; border-left: 5px solid #FF0B55; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; color: #ffffff; font-style: italic;">Frogwares' The Sinking City 2 is an Unreal Engine 5 survival horror game set in a flooded, 1920s Lovecraftian Arkham. Combining intense combat, resource management, and cosmic dread, it offers a thrilling evolution for fans of the genre.</div>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">✒️ DESCRIPTION</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Stepping away from the detective-adventure focus of the original game, The Sinking City 2 embraces a pure survival horror gameplay loop. Players explore the iconic, decay-ridden city of Arkham, fighting off eldritch abominations, managing scarce resources, and solving environmental puzzles. While investigation remains an optional tool to uncover hidden lore and secrets, the primary focus centers on terrifying encounters, resource management, and tense atmospheric exploration.</p>

<h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 2px solid #FF0B55; padding-bottom: 5px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase;">💥 GAMEPLAY FEATURES</h3>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Pure Survival Horror Mechanics: Experience inventory management, resource scarcity, tactical combat, and backtracking through complex, interconnected environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Unreal Engine 5 Visuals: Built from the ground up on modern technology, featuring realistic water physics, dynamic weather systems, and detailed body horror design.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Flooded World Exploration: Navigate the submerged streets of 1920s Arkham using boats and on foot as the water levels change and reveal new areas.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Visceral Arsenal & Combat: Utilize era-appropriate firearms and melee weapons to fend off horrific Lovecraftian monstrosities.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">Optional Investigation System: Uncover additional lore, side objectives, and gameplay advantages using an updated mental deduction mechanic without blocking main story progression.</p>
